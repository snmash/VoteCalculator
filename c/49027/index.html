Turnout in Millard County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,135 of Millard County’s 8,607 eligible citizens voted: 59.7% turnout. That’s 12.7 points higher than Utah overall (47.0%) and 13.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,472 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Millard County ranks #24 of 29 counties in Utah for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
