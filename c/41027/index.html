Turnout in Hood River County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,805 of Hood River County’s 16,606 eligible citizens voted: 65.1% turnout. That’s 4.3 points higher than Oregon overall (60.8%) and 19.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,801 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hood River County ranks #28 of 36 counties in Oregon for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
