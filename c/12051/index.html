Turnout in Hendry County

In the November 2022 midterm, 8,261 of Hendry County’s 25,168 eligible citizens voted: 32.8% turnout. That’s 15.2 points lower than Florida overall (48.0%) and 13.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 16,907 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 53.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hendry County ranks #2 of 67 counties in Florida for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
