Turnout in Clarke County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,179 of Clarke County’s 11,858 eligible citizens voted: 60.5% turnout. That’s 12.7 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 14.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,679 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 82.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clarke County ranks #127 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
