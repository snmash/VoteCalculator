Turnout in Crittenden County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,074 of Crittenden County’s 6,863 eligible citizens voted: 44.8% turnout. That’s 1.4 points higher than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 1.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,789 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Crittenden County ranks #77 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
