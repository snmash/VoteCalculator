Turnout in Hardin County

In the November 2022 midterm, 30,886 of Hardin County’s 82,255 eligible citizens voted: 37.5% turnout. That’s 5.9 points lower than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 8.5 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 51,369 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hardin County ranks #23 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
