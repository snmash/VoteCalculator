Turnout in Maries County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,454 of Maries County’s 6,700 eligible citizens voted: 51.6% turnout. That’s 7.5 points higher than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 5.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,246 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Maries County ranks #107 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
