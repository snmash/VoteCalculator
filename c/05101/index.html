Turnout in Newton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,080 of Newton County’s 5,768 eligible citizens voted: 53.4% turnout. That’s 13.0 points higher than Arkansas overall (40.4%) and 7.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,688 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Newton County ranks #75 of 75 counties in Arkansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
