Texas voter turnout by county

Each vote in Texas counts for 2.5 people

41% of eligible Texas citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 8,106,799 of Texas’s 19,866,015 eligible citizens voted (40.8%), so each ballot spoke for 2.5 people. In 2024, turnout was 57.3% (1.7 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Ector County (3.9 people per vote) and lowest in Loving County (1.0).
