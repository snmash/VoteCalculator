New Jersey voter turnout by county

Each vote in New Jersey counts for 2.5 people

41% of eligible New Jersey citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 2,609,652 of New Jersey’s 6,433,720 eligible citizens voted (40.6%), so each ballot spoke for 2.5 people. In 2024, turnout was 66.4% (1.5 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Hudson County (3.9 people per vote) and lowest in Hunterdon County (1.7).
