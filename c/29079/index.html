Turnout in Grundy County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,230 of Grundy County’s 7,206 eligible citizens voted: 44.8% turnout. That’s 0.7 points higher than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 1.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,976 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Grundy County ranks #55 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
