Turnout in Bradford County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,267 of Bradford County’s 21,945 eligible citizens voted: 46.8% turnout. That’s 1.2 points lower than Florida overall (48.0%) and 0.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 11,678 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bradford County ranks #29 of 67 counties in Florida for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
