Turnout in Lincoln County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,078 of Lincoln County’s 8,883 eligible citizens voted: 68.4% turnout. That’s 13.8 points higher than Washington overall (54.6%) and 22.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,805 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 80.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lincoln County ranks #36 of 39 counties in Washington for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
