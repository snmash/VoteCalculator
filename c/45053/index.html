Turnout in Jasper County

In the November 2022 midterm, 11,138 of Jasper County’s 24,053 eligible citizens voted: 46.3% turnout. That’s 3.7 points higher than South Carolina overall (42.6%) and about the same as the national rate (46.0%). With 12,915 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 29.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jasper County ranks #39 of 46 counties in South Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
