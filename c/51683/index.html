Turnout in Manassas city

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,653 of Manassas city’s 24,939 eligible citizens voted: 42.7% turnout. That’s 5.1 points lower than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 3.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 14,286 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Manassas city ranks #51 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
