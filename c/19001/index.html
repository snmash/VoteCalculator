Turnout in Adair County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,174 of Adair County’s 5,732 eligible citizens voted: 55.4% turnout. That’s 4.1 points higher than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 9.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,558 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Adair County ranks #75 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
