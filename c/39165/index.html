Turnout in Warren County

In the November 2022 midterm, 101,618 of Warren County’s 181,036 eligible citizens voted: 56.1% turnout. That’s 9.8 points higher than Ohio overall (46.3%) and 10.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 79,418 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Warren County ranks #83 of 88 counties in Ohio for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
