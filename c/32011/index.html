Turnout in Eureka County

In the November 2022 midterm, 778 of Eureka County’s 1,274 eligible citizens voted: 61.1% turnout. That’s 15.2 points higher than Nevada overall (45.9%) and 15.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 496 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 81.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Eureka County ranks #14 of 17 counties in Nevada for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
