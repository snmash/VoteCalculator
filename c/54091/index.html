Turnout in Taylor County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,354 of Taylor County’s 13,108 eligible citizens voted: 33.2% turnout. That’s about the same as West Virginia overall (33.5%) and 12.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,754 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 55.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Taylor County ranks #24 of 55 counties in West Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
