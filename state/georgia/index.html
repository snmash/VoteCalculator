Georgia voter turnout by county

Each vote in Georgia counts for 2.0 people

50% of eligible Georgia citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 3,953,408 of Georgia’s 7,834,481 eligible citizens voted (50.5%), so each ballot spoke for 2.0 people. In 2024, turnout was 67.0% (1.5 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Chattahoochee County (7.3 people per vote) and lowest in Oconee County (1.4).
