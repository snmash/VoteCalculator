Turnout in Pike County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,431 of Pike County’s 15,107 eligible citizens voted: 62.4% turnout. That’s 11.9 points higher than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 16.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,676 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 83.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pike County ranks #151 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
