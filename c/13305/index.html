Turnout in Wayne County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,025 of Wayne County’s 22,657 eligible citizens voted: 44.2% turnout. That’s 6.3 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 1.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 12,632 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Wayne County ranks #48 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
