Turnout in Faulk County

In the November 2022 midterm, 955 of Faulk County’s 1,585 eligible citizens voted: 60.3% turnout. That’s 8.0 points higher than South Dakota overall (52.3%) and 14.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 630 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Faulk County ranks #47 of 66 counties in South Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
