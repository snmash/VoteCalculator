Turnout in Andrews County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,826 of Andrews County’s 11,573 eligible citizens voted: 33.1% turnout. That’s 7.7 points lower than Texas overall (40.8%) and 12.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,747 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 52.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Andrews County ranks #31 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
