Turnout in Bennington County

In the November 2022 midterm, 15,908 of Bennington County’s 29,994 eligible citizens voted: 53.0% turnout. That’s 2.0 points lower than Vermont overall (55.0%) and 7.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 14,086 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bennington County ranks #5 of 14 counties in Vermont for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
