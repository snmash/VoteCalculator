Turnout in Craig County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,070 of Craig County’s 3,730 eligible citizens voted: 55.5% turnout. That’s 7.7 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 9.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,660 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 83.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 28.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Craig County ranks #109 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
