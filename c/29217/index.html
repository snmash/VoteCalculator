Turnout in Vernon County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,156 of Vernon County’s 14,741 eligible citizens voted: 41.8% turnout. That’s 2.3 points lower than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 4.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,585 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Vernon County ranks #36 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
