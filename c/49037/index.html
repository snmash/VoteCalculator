Turnout in San Juan County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,597 of San Juan County’s 10,264 eligible citizens voted: 54.5% turnout. That’s 7.5 points higher than Utah overall (47.0%) and 8.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,667 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 7.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

San Juan County ranks #16 of 29 counties in Utah for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
