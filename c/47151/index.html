Turnout in Scott County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,593 of Scott County’s 16,902 eligible citizens voted: 21.3% turnout. That’s 11.1 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 24.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 13,309 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 4.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 35.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Scott County ranks #2 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
