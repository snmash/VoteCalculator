Turnout in Jersey County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,273 of Jersey County’s 16,924 eligible citizens voted: 54.8% turnout. That’s 9.7 points higher than Illinois overall (45.1%) and 8.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,651 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jersey County ranks #82 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
