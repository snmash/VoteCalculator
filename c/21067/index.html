Turnout in Fayette County

In the November 2022 midterm, 106,548 of Fayette County’s 234,839 eligible citizens voted: 45.4% turnout. That’s 2.0 points higher than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 0.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 128,291 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Fayette County ranks #82 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
