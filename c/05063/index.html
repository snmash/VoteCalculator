Turnout in Independence County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,585 of Independence County’s 27,808 eligible citizens voted: 38.1% turnout. That’s 2.3 points lower than Arkansas overall (40.4%) and 7.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 17,223 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 50.4%, so each ballot spoke for 2.0 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Independence County ranks #25 of 75 counties in Arkansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
