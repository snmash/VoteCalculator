Turnout in Hampton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,638 of Hampton County’s 14,082 eligible citizens voted: 40.0% turnout. That’s 2.6 points lower than South Carolina overall (42.6%) and 6.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,444 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hampton County ranks #15 of 46 counties in South Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
