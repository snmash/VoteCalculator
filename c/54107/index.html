Turnout in Wood County

In the November 2022 midterm, 24,369 of Wood County’s 65,754 eligible citizens voted: 37.1% turnout. That’s 3.6 points higher than West Virginia overall (33.5%) and 8.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 41,385 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Wood County ranks #43 of 55 counties in West Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
