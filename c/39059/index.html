Turnout in Guernsey County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,227 of Guernsey County’s 29,595 eligible citizens voted: 41.3% turnout. That’s 5.0 points lower than Ohio overall (46.3%) and 4.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 17,368 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Guernsey County ranks #19 of 88 counties in Ohio for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
