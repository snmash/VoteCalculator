Idaho voter turnout by county

Each vote in Idaho counts for 2.4 people

42% of eligible Idaho citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 592,683 of Idaho’s 1,411,134 eligible citizens voted (42.0%), so each ballot spoke for 2.4 people. In 2024, turnout was 64.1% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Madison County (5.0 people per vote) and lowest in Camas County (1.6).
