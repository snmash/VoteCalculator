Turnout in Jefferson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,479 of Jefferson County’s 10,189 eligible citizens voted: 63.6% turnout. That’s 10.4 points higher than Montana overall (53.2%) and 17.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,710 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 81.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jefferson County ranks #47 of 56 counties in Montana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
