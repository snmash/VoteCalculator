New Hampshire voter turnout by county

Each vote in New Hampshire counts for 1.8 people

56% of eligible New Hampshire citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 621,041 of New Hampshire’s 1,109,609 eligible citizens voted (56.0%), so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. In 2024, turnout was 74.5% (1.3 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Coos County (2.0 people per vote) and lowest in Carroll County (1.6).
