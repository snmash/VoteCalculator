Turnout in Shelby County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,688 of Shelby County’s 16,235 eligible citizens voted: 59.7% turnout. That’s 14.6 points higher than Illinois overall (45.1%) and 13.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,547 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Shelby County ranks #98 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
