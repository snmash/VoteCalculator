Hawaii voter turnout by county

Each vote in Hawaii counts for 2.6 people

39% of eligible Hawaii citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 413,262 of Hawaii’s 1,054,561 eligible citizens voted (39.2%), so each ballot spoke for 2.6 people. In 2024, turnout was 49.0% (2.0 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Honolulu County (2.7 people per vote) and lowest in Kauai County (2.2).
