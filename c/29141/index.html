Turnout in Morgan County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,951 of Morgan County’s 16,690 eligible citizens voted: 41.6% turnout. That’s 2.5 points lower than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 4.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,739 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Morgan County ranks #30 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
