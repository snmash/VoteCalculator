Turnout in Houston County

In the November 2022 midterm, 27,625 of Houston County’s 81,549 eligible citizens voted: 33.9% turnout. That’s 2.9 points lower than Alabama overall (36.8%) and 12.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 53,924 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 54.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Houston County ranks #10 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
