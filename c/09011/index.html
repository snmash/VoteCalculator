Turnout in New London County

In the November 2022 midterm, 100,085 of New London County’s 208,652 eligible citizens voted: 48.0% turnout. That’s about the same as Connecticut overall (47.6%) and 2.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 108,567 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

New London County ranks #5 of 8 counties in Connecticut for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
