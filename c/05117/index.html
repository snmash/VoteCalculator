Turnout in Prairie County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,668 of Prairie County’s 6,395 eligible citizens voted: 41.7% turnout. That’s 1.3 points higher than Arkansas overall (40.4%) and 4.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,727 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 50.0%, so each ballot spoke for 2.0 people. Midterm turnout here ran 8.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Prairie County ranks #47 of 75 counties in Arkansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
