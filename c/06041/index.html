Turnout in Marin County

In the November 2022 midterm, 119,064 of Marin County’s 185,340 eligible citizens voted: 64.2% turnout. That’s 22.3 points higher than California overall (41.9%) and 18.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 66,276 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Marin County ranks #57 of 58 counties in California for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
