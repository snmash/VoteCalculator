Turnout in Fulton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,287 of Fulton County’s 11,630 eligible citizens voted: 54.1% turnout. That’s about the same as Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 8.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,343 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Fulton County ranks #44 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
