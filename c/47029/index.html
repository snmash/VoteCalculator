Turnout in Cocke County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,875 of Cocke County’s 29,144 eligible citizens voted: 27.0% turnout. That’s 5.4 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 19.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 21,269 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 53.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Cocke County ranks #15 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
