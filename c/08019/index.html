Turnout in Clear Creek County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,189 of Clear Creek County’s 7,855 eligible citizens voted: 66.1% turnout. That’s 8.7 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 20.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,666 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clear Creek County ranks #48 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
