Turnout in Okeechobee County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,886 of Okeechobee County’s 29,488 eligible citizens voted: 36.9% turnout. That’s 11.1 points lower than Florida overall (48.0%) and 9.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 18,602 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 54.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Okeechobee County ranks #4 of 67 counties in Florida for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
