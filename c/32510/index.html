Turnout in Carson City

In the November 2022 midterm, 23,694 of Carson City’s 43,180 eligible citizens voted: 54.9% turnout. That’s 9.0 points higher than Nevada overall (45.9%) and 8.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 19,486 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Carson City ranks #13 of 17 counties in Nevada for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
