Turnout in Grant Parish

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,350 of Grant Parish’s 16,818 eligible citizens voted: 37.8% turnout. That’s 2.7 points lower than Louisiana overall (40.5%) and 8.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 10,468 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 53.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Grant Parish ranks #12 of 64 counties in Louisiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
