Turnout in Seminole County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,864 of Seminole County’s 17,503 eligible citizens voted: 33.5% turnout. That’s 5.9 points lower than Oklahoma overall (39.4%) and 12.5 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 11,639 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 46.0%, so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Seminole County ranks #7 of 77 counties in Oklahoma for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
