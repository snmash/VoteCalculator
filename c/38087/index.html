Turnout in Slope County

In the November 2022 midterm, 303 of Slope County’s 644 eligible citizens voted: 47.0% turnout. That’s 5.7 points higher than North Dakota overall (41.3%) and 1.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 341 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Slope County ranks #19 of 53 counties in North Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
