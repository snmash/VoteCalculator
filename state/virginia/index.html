Virginia voter turnout by county

Each vote in Virginia counts for 2.1 people

48% of eligible Virginia citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 3,047,574 of Virginia’s 6,378,460 eligible citizens voted (47.8%), so each ballot spoke for 2.1 people. In 2024, turnout was 70.3% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Radford city (3.8 people per vote) and lowest in Rappahannock County (1.5).
