Turnout in Norton city

In the November 2022 midterm, 938 of Norton city’s 2,853 eligible citizens voted: 32.9% turnout. That’s 14.9 points lower than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 13.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,915 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Norton city ranks #13 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
