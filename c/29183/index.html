Turnout in St. Charles County

In the November 2022 midterm, 150,979 of St. Charles County’s 313,239 eligible citizens voted: 48.2% turnout. That’s 4.1 points higher than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 2.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 162,260 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

St. Charles County ranks #85 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
