Turnout in Jefferson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,785 of Jefferson County’s 45,456 eligible citizens voted: 28.1% turnout. That’s 4.3 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 17.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 32,671 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 28.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jefferson County ranks #21 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
