Turnout in Geneva County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,978 of Geneva County’s 20,708 eligible citizens voted: 38.5% turnout. That’s 1.7 points higher than Alabama overall (36.8%) and 7.5 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 12,730 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Geneva County ranks #39 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
