Turnout in Lipscomb County

In the November 2022 midterm, 978 of Lipscomb County’s 1,740 eligible citizens voted: 56.2% turnout. That’s 15.4 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 10.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 762 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lipscomb County ranks #222 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
