Turnout in Lumpkin County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,737 of Lumpkin County’s 28,308 eligible citizens voted: 45.0% turnout. That’s 5.5 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 1.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 15,571 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 62.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lumpkin County ranks #53 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
