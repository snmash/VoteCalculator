Turnout in Grant County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,953 of Grant County’s 4,657 eligible citizens voted: 63.4% turnout. That’s 4.3 points higher than Minnesota overall (59.1%) and 17.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,704 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Grant County ranks #81 of 87 counties in Minnesota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
