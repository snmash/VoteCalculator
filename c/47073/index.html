Turnout in Hawkins County

In the November 2022 midterm, 13,786 of Hawkins County’s 46,574 eligible citizens voted: 29.6% turnout. That’s 2.8 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 16.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 32,788 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hawkins County ranks #31 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
