Turnout in Clinch County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,114 of Clinch County’s 4,747 eligible citizens voted: 44.5% turnout. That’s 6.0 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 1.5 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,633 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clinch County ranks #51 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
