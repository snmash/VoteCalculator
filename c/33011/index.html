Turnout in Hillsborough County

In the November 2022 midterm, 172,376 of Hillsborough County’s 325,451 eligible citizens voted: 53.0% turnout. That’s 3.0 points lower than New Hampshire overall (56.0%) and 7.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 153,075 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hillsborough County ranks #3 of 10 counties in New Hampshire for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
