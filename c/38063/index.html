Turnout in Nelson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,518 of Nelson County’s 2,326 eligible citizens voted: 65.3% turnout. That’s 24.0 points higher than North Dakota overall (41.3%) and 19.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 808 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Nelson County ranks #53 of 53 counties in North Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
