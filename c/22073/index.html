Turnout in Ouachita Parish

In the November 2022 midterm, 42,920 of Ouachita Parish’s 117,787 eligible citizens voted: 36.4% turnout. That’s 4.1 points lower than Louisiana overall (40.5%) and 9.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 74,867 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 54.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Ouachita Parish ranks #10 of 64 counties in Louisiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
