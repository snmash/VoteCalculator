Turnout in Southampton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,409 of Southampton County’s 14,544 eligible citizens voted: 50.9% turnout. That’s 3.1 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 4.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,135 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Southampton County ranks #90 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
