Turnout in Mobile County

In the November 2022 midterm, 107,135 of Mobile County’s 310,889 eligible citizens voted: 34.5% turnout. That’s 2.3 points lower than Alabama overall (36.8%) and 11.5 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 203,754 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Mobile County ranks #20 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
