Maine voter turnout by county

Each vote in Maine counts for 1.7 people

61% of eligible Maine citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 674,981 of Maine’s 1,114,215 eligible citizens voted (60.6%), so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. In 2024, turnout was 74.0% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Aroostook County (1.9 people per vote) and lowest in Lincoln County (1.4).
