Turnout in Skagit County

In the November 2022 midterm, 56,842 of Skagit County’s 97,935 eligible citizens voted: 58.0% turnout. That’s 3.4 points higher than Washington overall (54.6%) and 12.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 41,093 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Skagit County ranks #24 of 39 counties in Washington for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
