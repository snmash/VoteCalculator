Turnout in Pasquotank County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,161 of Pasquotank County’s 31,679 eligible citizens voted: 44.7% turnout. That’s 3.1 points lower than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 1.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 17,518 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pasquotank County ranks #25 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
