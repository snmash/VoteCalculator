Turnout in Jasper County

In the November 2022 midterm, 15,013 of Jasper County’s 29,011 eligible citizens voted: 51.7% turnout. That’s about the same as Iowa overall (51.3%) and 5.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 13,998 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jasper County ranks #42 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
