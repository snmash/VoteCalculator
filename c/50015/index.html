Turnout in Lamoille County

In the November 2022 midterm, 11,551 of Lamoille County’s 20,449 eligible citizens voted: 56.5% turnout. That’s 1.5 points higher than Vermont overall (55.0%) and 10.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,898 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lamoille County ranks #9 of 14 counties in Vermont for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
