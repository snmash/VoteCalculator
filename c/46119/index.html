Turnout in Sully County

In the November 2022 midterm, 792 of Sully County’s 1,121 eligible citizens voted: 70.7% turnout. That’s 18.4 points higher than South Dakota overall (52.3%) and 24.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 329 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 80.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 9.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sully County ranks #62 of 66 counties in South Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
