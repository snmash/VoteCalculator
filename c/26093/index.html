Turnout in Livingston County

In the November 2022 midterm, 108,839 of Livingston County’s 154,489 eligible citizens voted: 70.5% turnout. That’s 12.4 points higher than Michigan overall (58.1%) and 24.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 45,650 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 85.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Livingston County ranks #79 of 83 counties in Michigan for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
