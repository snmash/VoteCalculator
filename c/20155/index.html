Turnout in Reno County

In the November 2022 midterm, 20,776 of Reno County’s 47,155 eligible citizens voted: 44.1% turnout. That’s 3.2 points lower than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 1.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 26,379 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Reno County ranks #24 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
