Turnout in Winkler County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,321 of Winkler County’s 4,587 eligible citizens voted: 28.8% turnout. That’s 12.0 points lower than Texas overall (40.8%) and 17.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,266 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 42.1%, so each ballot spoke for 2.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Winkler County ranks #12 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
