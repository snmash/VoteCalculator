Turnout in Mineral County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,925 of Mineral County’s 21,377 eligible citizens voted: 37.1% turnout. That’s 3.6 points higher than West Virginia overall (33.5%) and 8.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 13,452 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Mineral County ranks #42 of 55 counties in West Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
