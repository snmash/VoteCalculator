Turnout in Major County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,691 of Major County’s 5,494 eligible citizens voted: 49.0% turnout. That’s 9.6 points higher than Oklahoma overall (39.4%) and 3.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,803 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Major County ranks #72 of 77 counties in Oklahoma for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
