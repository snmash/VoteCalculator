Turnout in Trimble County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,190 of Trimble County’s 6,700 eligible citizens voted: 47.6% turnout. That’s 4.2 points higher than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 1.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,510 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Trimble County ranks #96 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
