Turnout in Union County

In the November 2022 midterm, 25,518 of Union County’s 49,310 eligible citizens voted: 51.8% turnout. That’s 5.5 points higher than Ohio overall (46.3%) and 5.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 23,792 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Union County ranks #80 of 88 counties in Ohio for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
