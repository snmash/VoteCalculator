Turnout in Putnam County

In the November 2022 midterm, 18,912 of Putnam County’s 62,986 eligible citizens voted: 30.0% turnout. That’s 2.4 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 16.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 44,074 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 55.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 25.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Putnam County ranks #35 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
