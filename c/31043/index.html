Turnout in Dakota County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,603 of Dakota County’s 12,824 eligible citizens voted: 28.1% turnout. That’s 19.2 points lower than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 17.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,221 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 47.7%, so each ballot spoke for 2.1 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Dakota County ranks #1 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
