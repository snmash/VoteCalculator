Turnout in Lynn County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,727 of Lynn County’s 4,011 eligible citizens voted: 43.1% turnout. That’s 2.3 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 2.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,284 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lynn County ranks #117 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
