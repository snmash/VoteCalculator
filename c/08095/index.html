Turnout in Phillips County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,981 of Phillips County’s 3,037 eligible citizens voted: 65.2% turnout. That’s 7.8 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 19.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,056 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Phillips County ranks #42 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
