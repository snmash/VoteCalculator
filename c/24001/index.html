Turnout in Allegany County

In the November 2022 midterm, 21,697 of Allegany County’s 54,805 eligible citizens voted: 39.6% turnout. That’s 5.8 points lower than Maryland overall (45.4%) and 6.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 33,108 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Allegany County ranks #4 of 24 counties in Maryland for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
