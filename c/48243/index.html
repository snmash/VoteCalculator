Turnout in Jeff Davis County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,051 of Jeff Davis County’s 1,455 eligible citizens voted: 72.2% turnout. That’s 31.4 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 26.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 404 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 80.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 8.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jeff Davis County ranks #247 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
