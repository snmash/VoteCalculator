Turnout in Delaware County

In the November 2022 midterm, 250,855 of Delaware County’s 430,723 eligible citizens voted: 58.2% turnout. That’s 4.3 points higher than Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 12.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 179,868 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Delaware County ranks #60 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
