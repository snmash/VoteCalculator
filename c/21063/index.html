Turnout in Elliott County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,064 of Elliott County’s 6,057 eligible citizens voted: 34.1% turnout. That’s 9.3 points lower than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 11.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,993 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 48.1%, so each ballot spoke for 2.1 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Elliott County ranks #9 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
