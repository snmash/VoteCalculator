Alabama voter turnout by county

Each vote in Alabama counts for 2.7 people

37% of eligible Alabama citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,415,715 of Alabama’s 3,845,715 eligible citizens voted (36.8%), so each ballot spoke for 2.7 people. In 2024, turnout was 58.9% (1.7 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Russell County (3.8 people per vote) and lowest in Lowndes County (1.9).
