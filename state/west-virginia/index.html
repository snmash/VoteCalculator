West Virginia voter turnout by county

Each vote in West Virginia counts for 3.0 people

33% of eligible West Virginia citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 471,967 of West Virginia’s 1,409,276 eligible citizens voted (33.5%), so each ballot spoke for 3.0 people. In 2024, turnout was 54.1% (1.8 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in McDowell County (4.6 people per vote) and lowest in Tucker County (2.2).
