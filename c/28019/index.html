Turnout in Choctaw County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,512 of Choctaw County’s 6,354 eligible citizens voted: 39.5% turnout. That’s 7.6 points higher than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 6.5 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,842 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Choctaw County ranks #68 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
