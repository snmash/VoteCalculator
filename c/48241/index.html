Turnout in Jasper County

In the November 2022 midterm, 11,385 of Jasper County’s 24,661 eligible citizens voted: 46.2% turnout. That’s 5.4 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and about the same as the national rate (46.0%). With 13,276 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jasper County ranks #151 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
