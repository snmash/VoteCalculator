Turnout in Musselshell County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,259 of Musselshell County’s 4,094 eligible citizens voted: 55.2% turnout. That’s 2.0 points higher than Montana overall (53.2%) and 9.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,835 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Musselshell County ranks #24 of 56 counties in Montana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
