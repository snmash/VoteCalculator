Turnout in Anderson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 21,034 of Anderson County’s 61,217 eligible citizens voted: 34.4% turnout. That’s 2.0 points higher than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 11.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 40,183 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 25.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Anderson County ranks #78 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
