Turnout in Adams County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,254 of Adams County’s 22,464 eligible citizens voted: 45.6% turnout. That’s 1.7 points lower than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and about the same as the national rate (46.0%). With 12,210 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Adams County ranks #16 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
