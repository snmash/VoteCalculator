Turnout in Pulaski County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,859 of Pulaski County’s 27,606 eligible citizens voted: 39.3% turnout. That’s 8.5 points lower than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 6.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 16,747 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pulaski County ranks #40 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
