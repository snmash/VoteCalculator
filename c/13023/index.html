Turnout in Bleckley County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,805 of Bleckley County’s 9,833 eligible citizens voted: 48.9% turnout. That’s 1.6 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 2.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,028 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bleckley County ranks #86 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
