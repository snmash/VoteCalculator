Turnout in Walsh County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,481 of Walsh County’s 7,787 eligible citizens voted: 44.7% turnout. That’s 3.4 points higher than North Dakota overall (41.3%) and 1.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,306 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Walsh County ranks #14 of 53 counties in North Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
