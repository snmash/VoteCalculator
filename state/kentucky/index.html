Kentucky voter turnout by county

Each vote in Kentucky counts for 2.3 people

43% of eligible Kentucky citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,477,693 of Kentucky’s 3,401,777 eligible citizens voted (43.4%), so each ballot spoke for 2.3 people. In 2024, turnout was 61.0% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Martin County (3.4 people per vote) and lowest in Carlisle County (1.7).
