Turnout in Goliad County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,370 of Goliad County’s 5,502 eligible citizens voted: 61.3% turnout. That’s 20.5 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 15.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,132 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Goliad County ranks #235 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
