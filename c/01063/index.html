Turnout in Greene County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,992 of Greene County’s 5,823 eligible citizens voted: 51.4% turnout. That’s 14.6 points higher than Alabama overall (36.8%) and 5.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,831 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Greene County ranks #64 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
