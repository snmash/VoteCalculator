Turnout in Goshen County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,852 of Goshen County’s 9,961 eligible citizens voted: 48.7% turnout. That’s 4.5 points higher than Wyoming overall (44.2%) and 2.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,109 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 62.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Goshen County ranks #11 of 23 counties in Wyoming for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
