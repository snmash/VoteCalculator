Turnout in Warren County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,157 of Warren County’s 4,112 eligible citizens voted: 52.5% turnout. That’s 2.0 points higher than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 6.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,955 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Warren County ranks #114 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
