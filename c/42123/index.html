Turnout in Warren County

In the November 2022 midterm, 16,092 of Warren County’s 30,360 eligible citizens voted: 53.0% turnout. That’s 0.9 points lower than Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 7.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 14,268 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 68.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Warren County ranks #37 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
