Turnout in Manistee County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,646 of Manistee County’s 21,001 eligible citizens voted: 60.2% turnout. That’s 2.1 points higher than Michigan overall (58.1%) and 14.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,355 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Manistee County ranks #52 of 83 counties in Michigan for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
