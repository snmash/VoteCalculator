Turnout in Middlesex County

In the November 2022 midterm, 605,766 of Middlesex County’s 1,151,646 eligible citizens voted: 52.6% turnout. That’s 4.4 points higher than Massachusetts overall (48.2%) and 6.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 545,880 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Middlesex County ranks #9 of 14 counties in Massachusetts for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
