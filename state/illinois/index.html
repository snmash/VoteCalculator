Illinois voter turnout by county

Each vote in Illinois counts for 2.2 people

45% of eligible Illinois citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 4,100,922 of Illinois’s 9,094,968 eligible citizens voted (45.1%), so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. In 2024, turnout was 61.9% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Brown County (2.7 people per vote) and lowest in Calhoun County (1.5).
