Turnout in Mountrail County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,621 of Mountrail County’s 6,555 eligible citizens voted: 40.0% turnout. That’s 1.3 points lower than North Dakota overall (41.3%) and 6.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,934 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 62.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Mountrail County ranks #11 of 53 counties in North Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
