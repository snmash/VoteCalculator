Turnout in Henrico County

In the November 2022 midterm, 125,184 of Henrico County’s 242,594 eligible citizens voted: 51.6% turnout. That’s 3.8 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 5.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 117,410 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Henrico County ranks #94 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
