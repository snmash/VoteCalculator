Turnout in Grafton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 42,772 of Grafton County’s 75,577 eligible citizens voted: 56.6% turnout. That’s 0.6 points higher than New Hampshire overall (56.0%) and 10.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 32,805 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Grafton County ranks #6 of 10 counties in New Hampshire for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
