Turnout in Piatt County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,617 of Piatt County’s 12,992 eligible citizens voted: 58.6% turnout. That’s 13.5 points higher than Illinois overall (45.1%) and 12.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,375 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Piatt County ranks #95 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
