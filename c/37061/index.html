Turnout in Duplin County

In the November 2022 midterm, 15,210 of Duplin County’s 33,135 eligible citizens voted: 45.9% turnout. That’s 1.9 points lower than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and about the same as the national rate (46.0%). With 17,925 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Duplin County ranks #38 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
