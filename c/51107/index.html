Turnout in Loudoun County

In the November 2022 midterm, 162,976 of Loudoun County’s 278,045 eligible citizens voted: 58.6% turnout. That’s 10.8 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 12.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 115,069 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 82.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Loudoun County ranks #117 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
