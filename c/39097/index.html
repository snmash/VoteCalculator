Turnout in Madison County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,191 of Madison County’s 35,180 eligible citizens voted: 40.3% turnout. That’s 6.0 points lower than Ohio overall (46.3%) and 5.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 20,989 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Madison County ranks #14 of 88 counties in Ohio for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
