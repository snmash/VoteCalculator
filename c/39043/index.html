Turnout in Erie County

In the November 2022 midterm, 28,924 of Erie County’s 59,007 eligible citizens voted: 49.0% turnout. That’s 2.7 points higher than Ohio overall (46.3%) and 3.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 30,083 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Erie County ranks #68 of 88 counties in Ohio for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
