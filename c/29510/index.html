Turnout in St. Louis city

In the November 2022 midterm, 84,096 of St. Louis city’s 225,260 eligible citizens voted: 37.3% turnout. That’s 6.8 points lower than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 8.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 141,164 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 51.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

St. Louis city ranks #10 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
