Montana voter turnout by county

Each vote in Montana counts for 1.9 people

53% of eligible Montana citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 463,632 of Montana’s 871,182 eligible citizens voted (53.2%), so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. In 2024, turnout was 69.2% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Glacier County (3.1 people per vote) and lowest in Garfield County (1.2).
