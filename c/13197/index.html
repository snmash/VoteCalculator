Turnout in Marion County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,846 of Marion County’s 5,598 eligible citizens voted: 50.8% turnout. That’s about the same as Georgia overall (50.5%) and 4.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,752 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Marion County ranks #106 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
