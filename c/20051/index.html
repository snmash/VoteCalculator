Turnout in Ellis County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,552 of Ellis County’s 22,631 eligible citizens voted: 46.6% turnout. That’s 0.7 points lower than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 0.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 12,079 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Ellis County ranks #36 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
