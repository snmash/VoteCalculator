Turnout in Gila County

In the November 2022 midterm, 22,573 of Gila County’s 43,147 eligible citizens voted: 52.3% turnout. That’s 3.9 points higher than Arizona overall (48.4%) and 6.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 20,574 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Gila County ranks #13 of 15 counties in Arizona for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
