Turnout in Montgomery County

In the November 2022 midterm, 343,371 of Montgomery County’s 691,364 eligible citizens voted: 49.7% turnout. That’s 4.3 points higher than Maryland overall (45.4%) and 3.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 347,993 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 25.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Montgomery County ranks #15 of 24 counties in Maryland for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
