Turnout in Jefferson Davis County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,208 of Jefferson Davis County’s 8,901 eligible citizens voted: 36.0% turnout. That’s 4.1 points higher than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 10.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,693 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jefferson Davis County ranks #52 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
