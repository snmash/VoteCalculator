Turnout in Terrell County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,656 of Terrell County’s 6,812 eligible citizens voted: 53.7% turnout. That’s 3.2 points higher than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 7.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,156 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Terrell County ranks #118 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
