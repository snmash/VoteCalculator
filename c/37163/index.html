Turnout in Sampson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 17,879 of Sampson County’s 40,548 eligible citizens voted: 44.1% turnout. That’s 3.7 points lower than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 1.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 22,669 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 25.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sampson County ranks #23 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
