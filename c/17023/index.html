Turnout in Clark County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,320 of Clark County’s 11,748 eligible citizens voted: 53.8% turnout. That’s 8.7 points higher than Illinois overall (45.1%) and 7.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,428 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clark County ranks #79 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
