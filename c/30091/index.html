Turnout in Sheridan County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,611 of Sheridan County’s 2,809 eligible citizens voted: 57.4% turnout. That’s 4.2 points higher than Montana overall (53.2%) and 11.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,198 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 68.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sheridan County ranks #30 of 56 counties in Montana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
