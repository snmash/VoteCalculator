Turnout in Lee County

In the November 2022 midterm, 40,593 of Lee County’s 135,079 eligible citizens voted: 30.1% turnout. That’s 6.7 points lower than Alabama overall (36.8%) and 15.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 94,486 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 54.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lee County ranks #3 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
