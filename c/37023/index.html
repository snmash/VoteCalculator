Turnout in Burke County

In the November 2022 midterm, 29,978 of Burke County’s 69,781 eligible citizens voted: 43.0% turnout. That’s 4.8 points lower than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 3.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 39,803 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Burke County ranks #20 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
