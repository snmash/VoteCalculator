Turnout in Lamar County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,377 of Lamar County’s 10,616 eligible citizens voted: 41.2% turnout. That’s 4.4 points higher than Alabama overall (36.8%) and 4.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,239 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lamar County ranks #48 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
