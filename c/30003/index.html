Turnout in Big Horn County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,246 of Big Horn County’s 8,795 eligible citizens voted: 36.9% turnout. That’s 16.3 points lower than Montana overall (53.2%) and 9.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,549 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 50.8%, so each ballot spoke for 2.0 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Big Horn County ranks #3 of 56 counties in Montana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
