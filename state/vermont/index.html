Vermont voter turnout by county

Each vote in Vermont counts for 1.8 people

55% of eligible Vermont citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 287,077 of Vermont’s 521,794 eligible citizens voted (55.0%), so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. In 2024, turnout was 71.5% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Orleans County (2.1 people per vote) and lowest in Grand Isle County (1.5).
