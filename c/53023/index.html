Turnout in Garfield County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,287 of Garfield County’s 1,859 eligible citizens voted: 69.2% turnout. That’s 14.6 points higher than Washington overall (54.6%) and 23.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 572 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 3.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Garfield County ranks #37 of 39 counties in Washington for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
