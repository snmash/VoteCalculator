Turnout in New York County

In the November 2022 midterm, 453,311 of New York County’s 1,204,577 eligible citizens voted: 37.6% turnout. That’s 4.3 points lower than New York overall (41.9%) and 8.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 751,266 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 54.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

New York County ranks #5 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
