Turnout in Lanier County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,638 of Lanier County’s 7,594 eligible citizens voted: 34.7% turnout. That’s 15.8 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 11.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,956 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 49.2%, so each ballot spoke for 2.0 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lanier County ranks #6 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
