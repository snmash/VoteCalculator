Turnout in Panola County

In the November 2022 midterm, 8,311 of Panola County’s 17,032 eligible citizens voted: 48.8% turnout. That’s 8.0 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 2.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,721 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Panola County ranks #175 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
