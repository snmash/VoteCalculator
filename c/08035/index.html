Turnout in Douglas County

In the November 2022 midterm, 190,913 of Douglas County’s 276,823 eligible citizens voted: 69.0% turnout. That’s 11.6 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 23.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 85,910 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 88.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.1 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Douglas County ranks #53 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
