Turnout in Cherokee County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,476 of Cherokee County’s 35,302 eligible citizens voted: 41.0% turnout. That’s about the same as Texas overall (40.8%) and 5.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 20,826 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Cherokee County ranks #96 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
