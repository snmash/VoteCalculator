Turnout in Lee County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,071 of Lee County’s 5,971 eligible citizens voted: 34.7% turnout. That’s 8.7 points lower than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 11.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,900 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 45.1%, so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lee County ranks #10 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
