Turnout in Dent County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,022 of Dent County’s 11,149 eligible citizens voted: 45.0% turnout. That’s 0.9 points higher than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 1.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,127 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Dent County ranks #57 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
