Turnout in Bureau County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,678 of Bureau County’s 25,652 eligible citizens voted: 49.4% turnout. That’s 4.3 points higher than Illinois overall (45.1%) and 3.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 12,974 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 62.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bureau County ranks #54 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
