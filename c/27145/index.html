Turnout in Stearns County

In the November 2022 midterm, 65,485 of Stearns County’s 117,709 eligible citizens voted: 55.6% turnout. That’s 3.5 points lower than Minnesota overall (59.1%) and 9.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 52,224 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Stearns County ranks #23 of 87 counties in Minnesota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
