Turnout in Vance County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,967 of Vance County’s 30,754 eligible citizens voted: 42.2% turnout. That’s 5.6 points lower than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 3.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 17,787 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Vance County ranks #15 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
