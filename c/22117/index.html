Turnout in Washington Parish

In the November 2022 midterm, 13,171 of Washington Parish’s 34,301 eligible citizens voted: 38.4% turnout. That’s 2.1 points lower than Louisiana overall (40.5%) and 7.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 21,130 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 53.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Washington Parish ranks #15 of 64 counties in Louisiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
