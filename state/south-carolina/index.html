South Carolina voter turnout by county

Each vote in South Carolina counts for 2.3 people

43% of eligible South Carolina citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,709,341 of South Carolina’s 4,009,578 eligible citizens voted (42.6%), so each ballot spoke for 2.3 people. In 2024, turnout was 63.6% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Allendale County (3.4 people per vote) and lowest in McCormick County (1.9).
