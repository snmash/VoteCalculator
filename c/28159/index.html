Turnout in Winston County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,323 of Winston County’s 13,636 eligible citizens voted: 39.0% turnout. That’s 7.1 points higher than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 7.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,313 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Winston County ranks #66 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
