Turnout in Washington County

In the November 2022 midterm, 59,086 of Washington County’s 107,970 eligible citizens voted: 54.7% turnout. That’s 11.4 points higher than Rhode Island overall (43.3%) and 8.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 48,884 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Washington County ranks #5 of 5 counties in Rhode Island for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
