Turnout in Washington County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,011 of Washington County’s 10,748 eligible citizens voted: 55.9% turnout. That’s 10.8 points higher than Illinois overall (45.1%) and 9.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,737 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Washington County ranks #86 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
