Turnout in Jeff Davis County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,543 of Jeff Davis County’s 10,416 eligible citizens voted: 43.6% turnout. That’s 6.9 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 2.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,873 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jeff Davis County ranks #43 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
