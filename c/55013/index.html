Turnout in Burnett County

In the November 2022 midterm, 8,205 of Burnett County’s 14,241 eligible citizens voted: 57.6% turnout. That’s 1.4 points lower than Wisconsin overall (59.0%) and 11.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,036 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Burnett County ranks #37 of 72 counties in Wisconsin for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
