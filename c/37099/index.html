Turnout in Jackson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,931 of Jackson County’s 35,357 eligible citizens voted: 42.2% turnout. That’s 5.6 points lower than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 3.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 20,426 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 62.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jackson County ranks #14 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
