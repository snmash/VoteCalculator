Turnout in Ramsey County

In the November 2022 midterm, 218,077 of Ramsey County’s 390,442 eligible citizens voted: 55.9% turnout. That’s 3.2 points lower than Minnesota overall (59.1%) and 9.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 172,365 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Ramsey County ranks #25 of 87 counties in Minnesota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
