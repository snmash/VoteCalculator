Turnout in Braxton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,281 of Braxton County’s 9,959 eligible citizens voted: 32.9% turnout. That’s 0.6 points lower than West Virginia overall (33.5%) and 13.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,678 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 53.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Braxton County ranks #22 of 55 counties in West Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
