Turnout in Fayette County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,544 of Fayette County’s 14,844 eligible citizens voted: 50.8% turnout. That’s 0.5 points lower than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 4.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,300 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 66.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Fayette County ranks #36 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
