Turnout in Palo Alto County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,602 of Palo Alto County’s 6,734 eligible citizens voted: 53.5% turnout. That’s 2.2 points higher than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 7.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,132 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Palo Alto County ranks #62 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
