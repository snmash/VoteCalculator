Pennsylvania voter turnout by county

Each vote in Pennsylvania counts for 1.9 people

54% of eligible Pennsylvania citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 5,369,703 of Pennsylvania’s 9,961,806 eligible citizens voted (53.9%), so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. In 2024, turnout was 70.6% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Forest County (2.9 people per vote) and lowest in Chester County (1.6).
