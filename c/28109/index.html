Turnout in Pearl River County

In the November 2022 midterm, 13,207 of Pearl River County’s 43,908 eligible citizens voted: 30.1% turnout. That’s 1.8 points lower than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 15.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 30,701 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pearl River County ranks #18 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
