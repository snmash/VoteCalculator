Turnout in Morris County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,244 of Morris County’s 4,183 eligible citizens voted: 53.6% turnout. That’s 6.3 points higher than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 7.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,939 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 66.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Morris County ranks #72 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
