Turnout in Chase County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,525 of Chase County’s 2,575 eligible citizens voted: 59.2% turnout. That’s 11.9 points higher than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 13.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,050 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Chase County ranks #73 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
