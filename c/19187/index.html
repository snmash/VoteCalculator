Turnout in Webster County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,051 of Webster County’s 28,302 eligible citizens voted: 42.6% turnout. That’s 8.7 points lower than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 3.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 16,251 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Webster County ranks #7 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
