Turnout in Choctaw County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,245 of Choctaw County’s 9,885 eligible citizens voted: 53.1% turnout. That’s 16.3 points higher than Alabama overall (36.8%) and 7.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,640 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Choctaw County ranks #66 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
