Turnout in Eagle County

In the November 2022 midterm, 22,195 of Eagle County’s 39,107 eligible citizens voted: 56.8% turnout. That’s 0.6 points lower than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 10.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 16,912 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Eagle County ranks #19 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
