Turnout in Lamar County

In the November 2022 midterm, 17,428 of Lamar County’s 48,758 eligible citizens voted: 35.7% turnout. That’s 3.8 points higher than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 10.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 31,330 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lamar County ranks #50 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
