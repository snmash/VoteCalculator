Michigan voter turnout by county

Each vote in Michigan counts for 1.7 people

58% of eligible Michigan citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 4,445,972 of Michigan’s 7,653,717 eligible citizens voted (58.1%), so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. In 2024, turnout was 74.0% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Luce County (2.2 people per vote) and lowest in Leelanau County (1.2).
