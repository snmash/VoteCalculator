Turnout in Owsley County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,763 of Owsley County’s 3,203 eligible citizens voted: 55.0% turnout. That’s 11.6 points higher than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 9.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,440 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 2.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Owsley County ranks #116 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
