Turnout in Tillamook County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,611 of Tillamook County’s 21,761 eligible citizens voted: 67.1% turnout. That’s 6.3 points higher than Oregon overall (60.8%) and 21.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,150 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 7.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Tillamook County ranks #32 of 36 counties in Oregon for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
