Turnout in Lackawanna County

In the November 2022 midterm, 89,073 of Lackawanna County’s 166,089 eligible citizens voted: 53.6% turnout. That’s about the same as Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 7.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 77,016 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lackawanna County ranks #40 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
