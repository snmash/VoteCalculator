Turnout in Jerauld County

In the November 2022 midterm, 866 of Jerauld County’s 1,322 eligible citizens voted: 65.5% turnout. That’s 13.2 points higher than South Dakota overall (52.3%) and 19.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 456 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jerauld County ranks #57 of 66 counties in South Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
