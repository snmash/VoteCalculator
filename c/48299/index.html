Turnout in Llano County

In the November 2022 midterm, 11,037 of Llano County’s 18,294 eligible citizens voted: 60.3% turnout. That’s 19.5 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 14.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,257 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Llano County ranks #232 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
