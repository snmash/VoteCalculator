Turnout in Greene County

In the November 2022 midterm, 20,165 of Greene County’s 39,070 eligible citizens voted: 51.6% turnout. That’s 9.7 points higher than New York overall (41.9%) and 5.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 18,905 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Greene County ranks #51 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
