Turnout in Spalding County

In the November 2022 midterm, 24,395 of Spalding County’s 51,482 eligible citizens voted: 47.4% turnout. That’s 3.1 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 1.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 27,087 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Spalding County ranks #75 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
