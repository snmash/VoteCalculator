Turnout in Modoc County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,412 of Modoc County’s 6,607 eligible citizens voted: 51.6% turnout. That’s 9.7 points higher than California overall (41.9%) and 5.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,195 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 9.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Modoc County ranks #42 of 58 counties in California for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
