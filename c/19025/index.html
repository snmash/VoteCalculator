Turnout in Calhoun County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,980 of Calhoun County’s 7,612 eligible citizens voted: 52.3% turnout. That’s 1.0 points higher than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 6.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,632 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Calhoun County ranks #50 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
