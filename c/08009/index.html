Turnout in Baca County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,748 of Baca County’s 2,564 eligible citizens voted: 68.2% turnout. That’s 10.8 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 22.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 816 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 78.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Baca County ranks #51 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
