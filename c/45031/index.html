Turnout in Darlington County

In the November 2022 midterm, 20,048 of Darlington County’s 48,161 eligible citizens voted: 41.6% turnout. That’s 1.0 points lower than South Carolina overall (42.6%) and 4.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 28,113 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Darlington County ranks #26 of 46 counties in South Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
