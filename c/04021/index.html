Turnout in Pinal County

In the November 2022 midterm, 144,612 of Pinal County’s 347,036 eligible citizens voted: 41.7% turnout. That’s 6.7 points lower than Arizona overall (48.4%) and 4.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 202,424 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pinal County ranks #4 of 15 counties in Arizona for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
