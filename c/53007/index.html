Turnout in Chelan County

In the November 2022 midterm, 34,249 of Chelan County’s 55,974 eligible citizens voted: 61.2% turnout. That’s 6.6 points higher than Washington overall (54.6%) and 15.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 21,725 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Chelan County ranks #28 of 39 counties in Washington for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
