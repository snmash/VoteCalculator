Turnout in Essex County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,220 of Essex County’s 8,571 eligible citizens voted: 49.2% turnout. That’s 1.4 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 3.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,351 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Essex County ranks #80 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
