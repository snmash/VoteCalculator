Turnout in Briscoe County

In the November 2022 midterm, 593 of Briscoe County’s 974 eligible citizens voted: 60.9% turnout. That’s 20.1 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 14.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 381 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Briscoe County ranks #233 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
