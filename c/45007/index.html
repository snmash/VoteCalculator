Turnout in Anderson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 63,791 of Anderson County’s 158,866 eligible citizens voted: 40.2% turnout. That’s 2.4 points lower than South Carolina overall (42.6%) and 5.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 95,075 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Anderson County ranks #17 of 46 counties in South Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
