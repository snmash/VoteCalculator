Turnout in Crook County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,065 of Crook County’s 21,028 eligible citizens voted: 66.9% turnout. That’s 6.1 points higher than Oregon overall (60.8%) and 20.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,963 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Crook County ranks #31 of 36 counties in Oregon for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
