Turnout in Jefferson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 18,190 of Jefferson County’s 45,420 eligible citizens voted: 40.0% turnout. That’s 6.5 points higher than West Virginia overall (33.5%) and 6.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 27,230 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jefferson County ranks #48 of 55 counties in West Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
