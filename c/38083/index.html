Turnout in Sheridan County

In the November 2022 midterm, 624 of Sheridan County’s 1,075 eligible citizens voted: 58.0% turnout. That’s 16.7 points higher than North Dakota overall (41.3%) and 12.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 451 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sheridan County ranks #43 of 53 counties in North Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
