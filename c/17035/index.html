Turnout in Cumberland County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,763 of Cumberland County’s 8,009 eligible citizens voted: 59.5% turnout. That’s 14.4 points higher than Illinois overall (45.1%) and 13.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,246 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Cumberland County ranks #97 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
