Turnout in Issaquena County

In the November 2022 midterm, 587 of Issaquena County’s 875 eligible citizens voted: 67.1% turnout. That’s 35.2 points higher than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 21.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 288 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 4.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Issaquena County ranks #82 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
