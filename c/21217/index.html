Turnout in Taylor County

In the November 2022 midterm, 8,996 of Taylor County’s 20,108 eligible citizens voted: 44.7% turnout. That’s 1.3 points higher than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 1.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 11,112 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Taylor County ranks #76 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
