Turnout in Berkshire County

In the November 2022 midterm, 49,030 of Berkshire County’s 105,339 eligible citizens voted: 46.5% turnout. That’s 1.7 points lower than Massachusetts overall (48.2%) and 0.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 56,309 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Berkshire County ranks #6 of 14 counties in Massachusetts for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
