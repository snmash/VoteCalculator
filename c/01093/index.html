Turnout in Marion County

In the November 2022 midterm, 8,409 of Marion County’s 22,873 eligible citizens voted: 36.8% turnout. That’s about the same as Alabama overall (36.8%) and 9.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 14,464 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Marion County ranks #27 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
