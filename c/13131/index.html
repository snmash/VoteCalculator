Turnout in Grady County

In the November 2022 midterm, 8,341 of Grady County’s 18,483 eligible citizens voted: 45.1% turnout. That’s 5.4 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 0.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 10,142 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Grady County ranks #54 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
