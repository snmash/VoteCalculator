Turnout in Phillips County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,366 of Phillips County’s 11,186 eligible citizens voted: 39.0% turnout. That’s 1.4 points lower than Arkansas overall (40.4%) and 7.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,820 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 44.1%, so each ballot spoke for 2.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 5.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Phillips County ranks #29 of 75 counties in Arkansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
