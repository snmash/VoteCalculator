Turnout in Clay County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,245 of Clay County’s 11,167 eligible citizens voted: 38.0% turnout. That’s 1.2 points higher than Alabama overall (36.8%) and 8.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,922 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clay County ranks #34 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
