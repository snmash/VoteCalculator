Turnout in Falls Church city

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,437 of Falls Church city’s 10,414 eligible citizens voted: 61.8% turnout. That’s 14.0 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 15.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,977 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 86.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Falls Church city ranks #130 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
