Turnout in Hancock County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,202 of Hancock County’s 13,489 eligible citizens voted: 53.4% turnout. That’s 8.3 points higher than Illinois overall (45.1%) and 7.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,287 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hancock County ranks #74 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
