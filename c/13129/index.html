Turnout in Gordon County

In the November 2022 midterm, 18,907 of Gordon County’s 42,954 eligible citizens voted: 44.0% turnout. That’s 6.5 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 2.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 24,047 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Gordon County ranks #44 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
