Turnout in Cibola County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,816 of Cibola County’s 20,291 eligible citizens voted: 33.6% turnout. That’s 12.1 points lower than New Mexico overall (45.7%) and 12.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 13,475 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 44.2%, so each ballot spoke for 2.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Cibola County ranks #3 of 33 counties in New Mexico for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
