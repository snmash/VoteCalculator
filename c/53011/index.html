Turnout in Clark County

In the November 2022 midterm, 205,603 of Clark County’s 378,085 eligible citizens voted: 54.4% turnout. That’s about the same as Washington overall (54.6%) and 8.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 172,482 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clark County ranks #14 of 39 counties in Washington for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
