Turnout in Stark County

In the November 2022 midterm, 133,932 of Stark County’s 289,977 eligible citizens voted: 46.2% turnout. That’s about the same as Ohio overall (46.3%) and about the same as the national rate (46.0%). With 156,045 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Stark County ranks #50 of 88 counties in Ohio for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
