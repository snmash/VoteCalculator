Turnout in Decatur County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,272 of Decatur County’s 9,055 eligible citizens voted: 36.1% turnout. That’s 3.7 points higher than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 9.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,783 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Decatur County ranks #85 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
