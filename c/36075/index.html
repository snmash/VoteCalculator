Turnout in Oswego County

In the November 2022 midterm, 40,281 of Oswego County’s 92,962 eligible citizens voted: 43.3% turnout. That’s 1.4 points higher than New York overall (41.9%) and 2.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 52,681 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Oswego County ranks #14 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
