Turnout in Dearborn County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,267 of Dearborn County’s 39,744 eligible citizens voted: 35.9% turnout. That’s 1.1 points lower than Indiana overall (37.0%) and 10.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 25,477 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 66.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 31.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Dearborn County ranks #27 of 92 counties in Indiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
