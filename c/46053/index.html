Turnout in Gregory County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,054 of Gregory County’s 3,028 eligible citizens voted: 67.8% turnout. That’s 15.5 points higher than South Dakota overall (52.3%) and 21.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 974 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 7.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Gregory County ranks #61 of 66 counties in South Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
