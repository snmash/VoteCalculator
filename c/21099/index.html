Turnout in Hart County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,226 of Hart County’s 14,760 eligible citizens voted: 42.2% turnout. That’s 1.2 points lower than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 3.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,534 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hart County ranks #51 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
