Turnout in Vernon County

In the November 2022 midterm, 13,201 of Vernon County’s 22,832 eligible citizens voted: 57.8% turnout. That’s 1.2 points lower than Wisconsin overall (59.0%) and 11.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,631 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Vernon County ranks #42 of 72 counties in Wisconsin for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
