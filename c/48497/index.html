Turnout in Wise County

In the November 2022 midterm, 25,821 of Wise County’s 53,169 eligible citizens voted: 48.6% turnout. That’s 7.8 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 2.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 27,348 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Wise County ranks #174 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
