Turnout in Leavenworth County

In the November 2022 midterm, 26,893 of Leavenworth County’s 62,343 eligible citizens voted: 43.1% turnout. That’s 4.2 points lower than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 2.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 35,450 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Leavenworth County ranks #16 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
