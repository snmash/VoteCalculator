Turnout in Schenectady County

In the November 2022 midterm, 54,552 of Schenectady County’s 120,658 eligible citizens voted: 45.2% turnout. That’s 3.3 points higher than New York overall (41.9%) and 0.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 66,106 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Schenectady County ranks #27 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
