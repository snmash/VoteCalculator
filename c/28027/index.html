Turnout in Coahoma County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,685 of Coahoma County’s 14,652 eligible citizens voted: 25.2% turnout. That’s 6.7 points lower than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 20.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 10,967 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 4.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 46.3%, so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Coahoma County ranks #6 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
