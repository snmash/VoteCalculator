Turnout in Greene County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,446 of Greene County’s 24,179 eligible citizens voted: 39.1% turnout. That’s 2.1 points higher than Indiana overall (37.0%) and 6.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 14,733 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Greene County ranks #65 of 92 counties in Indiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
