Turnout in Twin Falls County

In the November 2022 midterm, 22,993 of Twin Falls County’s 65,287 eligible citizens voted: 35.2% turnout. That’s 6.8 points lower than Idaho overall (42.0%) and 10.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 42,294 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Twin Falls County ranks #6 of 44 counties in Idaho for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
