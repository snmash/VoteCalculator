Delaware voter turnout by county

Each vote in Delaware counts for 2.4 people

42% of eligible Delaware citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 321,649 of Delaware’s 764,113 eligible citizens voted (42.1%), so each ballot spoke for 2.4 people. In 2024, turnout was 67.0% (1.5 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Kent County (2.6 people per vote) and lowest in Sussex County (2.0).
