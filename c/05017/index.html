Turnout in Chicot County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,406 of Chicot County’s 7,541 eligible citizens voted: 45.2% turnout. That’s 4.8 points higher than Arkansas overall (40.4%) and 0.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,135 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 46.3%, so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 1.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Chicot County ranks #64 of 75 counties in Arkansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
