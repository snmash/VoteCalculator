Turnout in Murray County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,251 of Murray County’s 10,403 eligible citizens voted: 40.9% turnout. That’s 1.5 points higher than Oklahoma overall (39.4%) and 5.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,152 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Murray County ranks #45 of 77 counties in Oklahoma for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
