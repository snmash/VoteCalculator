Turnout in Terrell County

In the November 2022 midterm, 426 of Terrell County’s 691 eligible citizens voted: 61.6% turnout. That’s 20.8 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 15.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 265 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 3.0 points above the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Terrell County ranks #236 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
