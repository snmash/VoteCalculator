Turnout in Covington County

In the November 2022 midterm, 11,274 of Covington County’s 29,075 eligible citizens voted: 38.8% turnout. That’s 2.0 points higher than Alabama overall (36.8%) and 7.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 17,801 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Covington County ranks #41 of 67 counties in Alabama for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
