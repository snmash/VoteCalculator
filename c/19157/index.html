Turnout in Poweshiek County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,628 of Poweshiek County’s 14,467 eligible citizens voted: 52.7% turnout. That’s 1.4 points higher than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 6.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,839 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Poweshiek County ranks #53 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
