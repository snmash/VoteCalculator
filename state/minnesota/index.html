Minnesota voter turnout by county

Each vote in Minnesota counts for 1.7 people

59% of eligible Minnesota citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 2,510,864 of Minnesota’s 4,247,048 eligible citizens voted (59.1%), so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. In 2024, turnout was 76.6% (1.3 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Mahnomen County (2.2 people per vote) and lowest in Cook County (1.5).
