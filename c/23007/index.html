Turnout in Franklin County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,928 of Franklin County’s 24,911 eligible citizens voted: 59.9% turnout. That’s 0.7 points lower than Maine overall (60.6%) and 13.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,983 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Franklin County ranks #10 of 16 counties in Maine for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
