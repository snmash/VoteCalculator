Turnout in Hernando County

In the November 2022 midterm, 80,415 of Hernando County’s 163,461 eligible citizens voted: 49.2% turnout. That’s 1.2 points higher than Florida overall (48.0%) and 3.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 83,046 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hernando County ranks #35 of 67 counties in Florida for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
