Turnout in Sullivan County

In the November 2022 midterm, 24,787 of Sullivan County’s 58,138 eligible citizens voted: 42.6% turnout. That’s 0.7 points higher than New York overall (41.9%) and 3.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 33,351 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sullivan County ranks #11 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
