Turnout in Calvert County

In the November 2022 midterm, 37,577 of Calvert County’s 71,587 eligible citizens voted: 52.5% turnout. That’s 7.1 points higher than Maryland overall (45.4%) and 6.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 34,010 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Calvert County ranks #18 of 24 counties in Maryland for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
