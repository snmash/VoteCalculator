Turnout in Petroleum County

In the November 2022 midterm, 289 of Petroleum County’s 383 eligible citizens voted: 75.5% turnout. That’s 22.3 points higher than Montana overall (53.2%) and 29.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 94 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 84.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 9.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Petroleum County ranks #55 of 56 counties in Montana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
