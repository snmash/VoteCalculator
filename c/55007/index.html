Turnout in Bayfield County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,290 of Bayfield County’s 13,801 eligible citizens voted: 67.3% turnout. That’s 8.3 points higher than Wisconsin overall (59.0%) and 21.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,511 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 81.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bayfield County ranks #67 of 72 counties in Wisconsin for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
