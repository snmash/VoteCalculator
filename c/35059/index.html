Turnout in Union County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,508 of Union County’s 3,063 eligible citizens voted: 49.2% turnout. That’s 3.5 points higher than New Mexico overall (45.7%) and 3.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,555 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 54.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 5.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Union County ranks #19 of 33 counties in New Mexico for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
