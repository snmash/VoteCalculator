New York voter turnout by county

Each vote in New York counts for 2.4 people

42% of eligible New York citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 5,912,147 of New York’s 14,104,693 eligible citizens voted (41.9%), so each ballot spoke for 2.4 people. In 2024, turnout was 58.6% (1.7 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Bronx County (4.3 people per vote) and lowest in Hamilton County (1.5).
