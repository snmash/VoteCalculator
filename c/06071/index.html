Turnout in San Bernardino County

In the November 2022 midterm, 454,500 of San Bernardino County’s 1,425,320 eligible citizens voted: 31.9% turnout. That’s 10.0 points lower than California overall (41.9%) and 14.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 970,820 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 53.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

San Bernardino County ranks #3 of 58 counties in California for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
