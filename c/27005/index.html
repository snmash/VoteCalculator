Turnout in Becker County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,747 of Becker County’s 26,927 eligible citizens voted: 54.8% turnout. That’s 4.3 points lower than Minnesota overall (59.1%) and 8.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 12,180 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Becker County ranks #20 of 87 counties in Minnesota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
