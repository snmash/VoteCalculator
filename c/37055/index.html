Turnout in Dare County

In the November 2022 midterm, 17,844 of Dare County’s 30,212 eligible citizens voted: 59.1% turnout. That’s 11.3 points higher than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 13.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 12,368 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 83.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Dare County ranks #98 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
