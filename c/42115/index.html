Turnout in Susquehanna County

In the November 2022 midterm, 17,316 of Susquehanna County’s 30,731 eligible citizens voted: 56.3% turnout. That’s 2.4 points higher than Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 10.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 13,415 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Susquehanna County ranks #56 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
