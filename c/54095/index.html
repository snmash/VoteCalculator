Turnout in Tyler County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,385 of Tyler County’s 6,501 eligible citizens voted: 36.7% turnout. That’s 3.2 points higher than West Virginia overall (33.5%) and 9.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,116 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 55.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Tyler County ranks #40 of 55 counties in West Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
