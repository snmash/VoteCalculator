Turnout in Clark County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,805 of Clark County’s 28,672 eligible citizens voted: 44.7% turnout. That’s 1.3 points higher than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 1.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 15,867 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 62.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clark County ranks #74 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
