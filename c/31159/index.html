Turnout in Seward County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,721 of Seward County’s 13,440 eligible citizens voted: 50.0% turnout. That’s 2.7 points higher than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 4.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,719 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 68.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Seward County ranks #34 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
