Turnout in Matagorda County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,724 of Matagorda County’s 24,649 eligible citizens voted: 39.4% turnout. That’s 1.4 points lower than Texas overall (40.8%) and 6.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 14,925 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 54.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Matagorda County ranks #78 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
