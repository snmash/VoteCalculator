Turnout in Deaf Smith County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,947 of Deaf Smith County’s 10,445 eligible citizens voted: 28.2% turnout. That’s 12.6 points lower than Texas overall (40.8%) and 17.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,498 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 41.0%, so each ballot spoke for 2.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Deaf Smith County ranks #9 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
