Turnout in District of Columbia

In the November 2022 midterm, 201,330 of District of Columbia’s 506,637 eligible citizens voted: 39.7% turnout. That’s about the same as District of Columbia overall (39.7%) and 6.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 305,307 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.
