Turnout in Grand Traverse County

In the November 2022 midterm, 52,301 of Grand Traverse County’s 76,856 eligible citizens voted: 68.1% turnout. That’s 10.0 points higher than Michigan overall (58.1%) and 22.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 24,555 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 81.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Grand Traverse County ranks #75 of 83 counties in Michigan for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
