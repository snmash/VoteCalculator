Arkansas voter turnout by county

Each vote in Arkansas counts for 2.5 people

40% of eligible Arkansas citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 907,069 of Arkansas’s 2,245,940 eligible citizens voted (40.4%), so each ballot spoke for 2.5 people. In 2024, turnout was 52.7% (1.9 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Lincoln County (3.8 people per vote) and lowest in Newton County (1.9).
