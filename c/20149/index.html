Turnout in Pottawatomie County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,503 of Pottawatomie County’s 18,362 eligible citizens voted: 57.2% turnout. That’s 9.9 points higher than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 11.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,859 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pottawatomie County ranks #90 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
