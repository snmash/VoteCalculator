Turnout in Broward County

In the November 2022 midterm, 598,607 of Broward County’s 1,315,339 eligible citizens voted: 45.5% turnout. That’s 2.5 points lower than Florida overall (48.0%) and about the same as the national rate (46.0%). With 716,732 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 66.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Broward County ranks #23 of 67 counties in Florida for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
