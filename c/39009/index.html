Turnout in Athens County

In the November 2022 midterm, 19,081 of Athens County’s 51,199 eligible citizens voted: 37.3% turnout. That’s 9.0 points lower than Ohio overall (46.3%) and 8.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 32,118 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 50.3%, so each ballot spoke for 2.0 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Athens County ranks #3 of 88 counties in Ohio for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
