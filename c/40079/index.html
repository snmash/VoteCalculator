Turnout in Le Flore County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,141 of Le Flore County’s 35,963 eligible citizens voted: 33.8% turnout. That’s 5.6 points lower than Oklahoma overall (39.4%) and 12.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 23,822 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 52.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Le Flore County ranks #8 of 77 counties in Oklahoma for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
