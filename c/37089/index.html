Turnout in Henderson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 51,198 of Henderson County’s 91,032 eligible citizens voted: 56.2% turnout. That’s 8.4 points higher than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 10.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 39,834 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Henderson County ranks #90 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
