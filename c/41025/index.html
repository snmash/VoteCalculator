Turnout in Harney County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,834 of Harney County’s 6,000 eligible citizens voted: 63.9% turnout. That’s 3.1 points higher than Oregon overall (60.8%) and 17.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,166 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 7.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Harney County ranks #23 of 36 counties in Oregon for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
