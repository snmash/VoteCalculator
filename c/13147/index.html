Turnout in Hart County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,560 of Hart County’s 20,849 eligible citizens voted: 50.6% turnout. That’s about the same as Georgia overall (50.5%) and 4.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 10,289 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 68.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hart County ranks #104 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
