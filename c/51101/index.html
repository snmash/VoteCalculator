Turnout in King William County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,549 of King William County’s 14,274 eligible citizens voted: 52.9% turnout. That’s 5.1 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 6.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,725 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 81.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 28.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

King William County ranks #100 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
