Turnout in Wyoming County

In the November 2022 midterm, 11,779 of Wyoming County’s 20,775 eligible citizens voted: 56.7% turnout. That’s 2.8 points higher than Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 10.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,996 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Wyoming County ranks #58 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
