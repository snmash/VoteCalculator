North Carolina voter turnout by county

Each vote in North Carolina counts for 2.1 people

48% of eligible North Carolina citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 3,773,785 of North Carolina’s 7,898,142 eligible citizens voted (47.8%), so each ballot spoke for 2.1 people. In 2024, turnout was 72.2% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Onslow County (3.5 people per vote) and lowest in Chatham County (1.5).
