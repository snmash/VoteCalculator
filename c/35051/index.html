Turnout in Sierra County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,919 of Sierra County’s 9,095 eligible citizens voted: 54.1% turnout. That’s 8.4 points higher than New Mexico overall (45.7%) and 8.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,176 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sierra County ranks #26 of 33 counties in New Mexico for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
