Turnout in Alexandria city

In the November 2022 midterm, 54,223 of Alexandria city’s 111,276 eligible citizens voted: 48.7% turnout. That’s 0.9 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 2.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 57,053 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Alexandria city ranks #76 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
