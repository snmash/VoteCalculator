Turnout in Carbon County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,703 of Carbon County’s 10,862 eligible citizens voted: 43.3% turnout. That’s 0.9 points lower than Wyoming overall (44.2%) and 2.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,159 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Carbon County ranks #6 of 23 counties in Wyoming for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
