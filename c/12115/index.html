Turnout in Sarasota County

In the November 2022 midterm, 218,897 of Sarasota County’s 376,433 eligible citizens voted: 58.2% turnout. That’s 10.2 points higher than Florida overall (48.0%) and 12.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 157,536 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sarasota County ranks #63 of 67 counties in Florida for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
