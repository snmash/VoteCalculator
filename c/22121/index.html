Turnout in West Baton Rouge Parish

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,104 of West Baton Rouge Parish’s 20,869 eligible citizens voted: 48.4% turnout. That’s 7.9 points higher than Louisiana overall (40.5%) and 2.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 10,765 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 66.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

West Baton Rouge Parish ranks #56 of 64 counties in Louisiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
