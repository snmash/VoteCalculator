Turnout in Cheyenne County

In the November 2022 midterm, 917 of Cheyenne County’s 1,318 eligible citizens voted: 69.6% turnout. That’s 12.2 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 23.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 401 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 80.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Cheyenne County ranks #56 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
