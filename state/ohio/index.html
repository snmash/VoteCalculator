Ohio voter turnout by county

Each vote in Ohio counts for 2.2 people

46% of eligible Ohio citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 4,147,765 of Ohio’s 8,956,187 eligible citizens voted (46.3%), so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. In 2024, turnout was 64.4% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Holmes County (3.6 people per vote) and lowest in Delaware County (1.6).
