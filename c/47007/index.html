Turnout in Bledsoe County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,358 of Bledsoe County’s 12,575 eligible citizens voted: 26.7% turnout. That’s 5.7 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 19.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,217 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 49.2%, so each ballot spoke for 2.0 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bledsoe County ranks #14 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
