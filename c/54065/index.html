Turnout in Morgan County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,129 of Morgan County’s 14,380 eligible citizens voted: 35.7% turnout. That’s 2.2 points higher than West Virginia overall (33.5%) and 10.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,251 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 27.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Morgan County ranks #36 of 55 counties in West Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
