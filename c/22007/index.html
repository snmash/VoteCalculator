Turnout in Assumption Parish

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,655 of Assumption Parish’s 16,086 eligible citizens voted: 47.6% turnout. That’s 7.1 points higher than Louisiana overall (40.5%) and 1.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,431 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Assumption Parish ranks #51 of 64 counties in Louisiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
