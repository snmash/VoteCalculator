Turnout in Orange County

In the November 2022 midterm, 13,941 of Orange County’s 24,328 eligible citizens voted: 57.3% turnout. That’s 2.3 points higher than Vermont overall (55.0%) and 11.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 10,387 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Orange County ranks #10 of 14 counties in Vermont for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
