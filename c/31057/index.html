Turnout in Dundy County

In the November 2022 midterm, 753 of Dundy County’s 1,373 eligible citizens voted: 54.8% turnout. That’s 7.5 points higher than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 8.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 620 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Dundy County ranks #55 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
