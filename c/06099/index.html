Turnout in Stanislaus County

In the November 2022 midterm, 130,967 of Stanislaus County’s 349,960 eligible citizens voted: 37.4% turnout. That’s 4.5 points lower than California overall (41.9%) and 8.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 218,993 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Stanislaus County ranks #13 of 58 counties in California for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
