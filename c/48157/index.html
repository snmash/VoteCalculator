Turnout in Fort Bend County

In the November 2022 midterm, 250,204 of Fort Bend County’s 558,514 eligible citizens voted: 44.8% turnout. That’s 4.0 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 1.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 308,310 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Fort Bend County ranks #131 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
