Turnout in Pushmataha County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,297 of Pushmataha County’s 8,395 eligible citizens voted: 39.3% turnout. That’s about the same as Oklahoma overall (39.4%) and 6.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,098 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pushmataha County ranks #37 of 77 counties in Oklahoma for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
