Turnout in Parke County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,481 of Parke County’s 12,700 eligible citizens voted: 35.3% turnout. That’s 1.7 points lower than Indiana overall (37.0%) and 10.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,219 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 52.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Parke County ranks #24 of 92 counties in Indiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
