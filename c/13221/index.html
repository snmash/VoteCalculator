Turnout in Oglethorpe County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,811 of Oglethorpe County’s 11,772 eligible citizens voted: 57.9% turnout. That’s 7.4 points higher than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 11.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,961 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Oglethorpe County ranks #141 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
