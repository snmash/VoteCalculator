Wisconsin voter turnout by county

Each vote in Wisconsin counts for 1.7 people

59% of eligible Wisconsin citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 2,658,050 of Wisconsin’s 4,505,040 eligible citizens voted (59.0%), so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. In 2024, turnout was 76.0% (1.3 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Menominee County (2.4 people per vote) and lowest in Ozaukee County (1.4).
