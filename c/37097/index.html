Turnout in Iredell County

In the November 2022 midterm, 69,625 of Iredell County’s 145,798 eligible citizens voted: 47.8% turnout. That’s about the same as North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 1.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 76,173 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 28.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Iredell County ranks #49 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
