Turnout in Bristol County

In the November 2022 midterm, 183,390 of Bristol County’s 433,863 eligible citizens voted: 42.3% turnout. That’s 5.9 points lower than Massachusetts overall (48.2%) and 3.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 250,473 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 64.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bristol County ranks #3 of 14 counties in Massachusetts for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
