Turnout in Greenwood County

In the November 2022 midterm, 21,945 of Greenwood County’s 52,499 eligible citizens voted: 41.8% turnout. That’s 0.8 points lower than South Carolina overall (42.6%) and 4.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 30,554 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Greenwood County ranks #27 of 46 counties in South Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
