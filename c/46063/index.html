Turnout in Harding County

In the November 2022 midterm, 679 of Harding County’s 877 eligible citizens voted: 77.4% turnout. That’s 25.1 points higher than South Dakota overall (52.3%) and 31.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 198 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 93.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.1 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Harding County ranks #66 of 66 counties in South Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
