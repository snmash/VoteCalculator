Turnout in Tolland County

In the November 2022 midterm, 59,833 of Tolland County’s 118,039 eligible citizens voted: 50.7% turnout. That’s 3.1 points higher than Connecticut overall (47.6%) and 4.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 58,206 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Tolland County ranks #6 of 8 counties in Connecticut for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
