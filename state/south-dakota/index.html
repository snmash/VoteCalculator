South Dakota voter turnout by county

Each vote in South Dakota counts for 1.9 people

52% of eligible South Dakota citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 350,299 of South Dakota’s 669,727 eligible citizens voted (52.3%), so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. In 2024, turnout was 64.0% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Oglala Lakota County (3.5 people per vote) and lowest in Harding County (1.3).
