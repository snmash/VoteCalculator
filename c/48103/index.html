Turnout in Crane County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,147 of Crane County’s 2,444 eligible citizens voted: 46.9% turnout. That’s 6.1 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 0.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,297 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 9.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Crane County ranks #154 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
