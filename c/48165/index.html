Turnout in Gaines County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,116 of Gaines County’s 10,613 eligible citizens voted: 38.8% turnout. That’s 2.0 points lower than Texas overall (40.8%) and 7.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,497 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Gaines County ranks #68 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
