Turnout in Alpine County

In the November 2022 midterm, 619 of Alpine County’s 1,273 eligible citizens voted: 48.6% turnout. That’s 6.7 points higher than California overall (41.9%) and 2.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 654 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 9.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Alpine County ranks #36 of 58 counties in California for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
