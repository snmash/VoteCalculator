Turnout in Morrill County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,955 of Morrill County’s 3,433 eligible citizens voted: 56.9% turnout. That’s 9.6 points higher than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 10.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,478 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Morrill County ranks #66 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
