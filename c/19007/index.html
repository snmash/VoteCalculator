Turnout in Appanoose County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,711 of Appanoose County’s 9,452 eligible citizens voted: 49.8% turnout. That’s 1.5 points lower than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 3.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,741 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Appanoose County ranks #30 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
