Utah voter turnout by county

Each vote in Utah counts for 2.1 people

47% of eligible Utah citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,076,553 of Utah’s 2,291,421 eligible citizens voted (47.0%), so each ballot spoke for 2.1 people. In 2024, turnout was 65.0% (1.5 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Iron County (2.6 people per vote) and lowest in Daggett County (1.2).
