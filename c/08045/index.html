Turnout in Garfield County

In the November 2022 midterm, 24,515 of Garfield County’s 42,186 eligible citizens voted: 58.1% turnout. That’s 0.7 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 12.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 17,671 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Garfield County ranks #22 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
