Turnout in Taliaferro County

In the November 2022 midterm, 807 of Taliaferro County’s 1,261 eligible citizens voted: 64.0% turnout. That’s 13.5 points higher than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 18.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 454 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 6.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Taliaferro County ranks #154 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
