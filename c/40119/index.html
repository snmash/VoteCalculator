Turnout in Payne County

In the November 2022 midterm, 22,138 of Payne County’s 63,932 eligible citizens voted: 34.6% turnout. That’s 4.8 points lower than Oklahoma overall (39.4%) and 11.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 41,794 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 45.8%, so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Payne County ranks #12 of 77 counties in Oklahoma for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
