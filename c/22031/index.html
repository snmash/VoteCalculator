Turnout in De Soto Parish

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,247 of De Soto Parish’s 20,386 eligible citizens voted: 45.4% turnout. That’s 4.9 points higher than Louisiana overall (40.5%) and 0.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 11,139 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 68.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

De Soto Parish ranks #44 of 64 counties in Louisiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
