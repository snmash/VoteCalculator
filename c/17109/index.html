Turnout in McDonough County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,390 of McDonough County’s 21,612 eligible citizens voted: 43.4% turnout. That’s 1.7 points lower than Illinois overall (45.1%) and 2.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 12,222 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 55.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

McDonough County ranks #8 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
