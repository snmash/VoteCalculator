Turnout in Elk County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,076 of Elk County’s 1,875 eligible citizens voted: 57.4% turnout. That’s 10.1 points higher than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 11.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 799 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Elk County ranks #92 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
