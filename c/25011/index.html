Turnout in Franklin County

In the November 2022 midterm, 31,615 of Franklin County’s 57,530 eligible citizens voted: 55.0% turnout. That’s 6.8 points higher than Massachusetts overall (48.2%) and 9.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 25,915 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Franklin County ranks #12 of 14 counties in Massachusetts for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
