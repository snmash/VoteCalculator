Nevada voter turnout by county

Each vote in Nevada counts for 2.2 people

46% of eligible Nevada citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,020,486 of Nevada’s 2,223,532 eligible citizens voted (45.9%), so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. In 2024, turnout was 66.8% (1.5 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Pershing County (2.9 people per vote) and lowest in Storey County (1.4).
