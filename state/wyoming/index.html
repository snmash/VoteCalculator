Wyoming voter turnout by county

Each vote in Wyoming counts for 2.3 people

44% of eligible Wyoming citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 194,512 of Wyoming’s 440,304 eligible citizens voted (44.2%), so each ballot spoke for 2.3 people. In 2024, turnout was 61.1% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Campbell County (2.7 people per vote) and lowest in Hot Springs County (1.6).
