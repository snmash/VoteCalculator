Turnout in Houston County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,442 of Houston County’s 6,589 eligible citizens voted: 37.1% turnout. That’s 4.7 points higher than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 8.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,147 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Houston County ranks #87 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
