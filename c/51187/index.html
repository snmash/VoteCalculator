Turnout in Warren County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,248 of Warren County’s 31,282 eligible citizens voted: 45.5% turnout. That’s 2.3 points lower than Virginia overall (47.8%) and about the same as the national rate (46.0%). With 17,034 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Warren County ranks #62 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
