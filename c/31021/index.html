Turnout in Burt County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,790 of Burt County’s 5,154 eligible citizens voted: 54.1% turnout. That’s 6.8 points higher than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 8.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,364 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Burt County ranks #52 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
