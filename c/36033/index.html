Turnout in Franklin County

In the November 2022 midterm, 15,214 of Franklin County’s 36,866 eligible citizens voted: 41.3% turnout. That’s 0.6 points lower than New York overall (41.9%) and 4.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 21,652 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 52.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Franklin County ranks #8 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
