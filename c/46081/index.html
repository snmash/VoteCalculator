Turnout in Lawrence County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,284 of Lawrence County’s 22,626 eligible citizens voted: 54.3% turnout. That’s 2.0 points higher than South Dakota overall (52.3%) and 8.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 10,342 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 68.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lawrence County ranks #29 of 66 counties in South Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
