Turnout in Ozark County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,699 of Ozark County’s 7,174 eligible citizens voted: 51.6% turnout. That’s 7.5 points higher than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 5.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,475 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 66.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Ozark County ranks #108 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
