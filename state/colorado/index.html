Colorado voter turnout by county

Each vote in Colorado counts for 1.7 people

57% of eligible Colorado citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 2,500,221 of Colorado’s 4,355,191 eligible citizens voted (57.4%), so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. In 2024, turnout was 73.3% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Crowley County (3.5 people per vote) and lowest in Mineral County (1.0).
