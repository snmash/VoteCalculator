Indiana voter turnout by county

Each vote in Indiana counts for 2.7 people

37% of eligible Indiana citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,860,144 of Indiana’s 5,030,929 eligible citizens voted (37.0%), so each ballot spoke for 2.7 people. In 2024, turnout was 58.4% (1.7 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in LaGrange County (4.1 people per vote) and lowest in Hamilton County (2.0).
