Turnout in Davidson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 59,260 of Davidson County’s 130,997 eligible citizens voted: 45.2% turnout. That’s 2.6 points lower than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 0.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 71,737 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Davidson County ranks #29 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
