Turnout in Grant County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,613 of Grant County’s 3,902 eligible citizens voted: 41.3% turnout. That’s 6.0 points lower than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 4.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,289 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Grant County ranks #10 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
