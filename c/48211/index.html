Turnout in Hemphill County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,361 of Hemphill County’s 2,283 eligible citizens voted: 59.6% turnout. That’s 18.8 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 13.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 922 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hemphill County ranks #231 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
