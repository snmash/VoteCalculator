Turnout in Linn County

In the November 2022 midterm, 61,495 of Linn County’s 98,772 eligible citizens voted: 62.3% turnout. That’s 1.5 points higher than Oregon overall (60.8%) and 16.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 37,277 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Linn County ranks #20 of 36 counties in Oregon for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
