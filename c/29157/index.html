Turnout in Perry County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,340 of Perry County’s 14,512 eligible citizens voted: 43.7% turnout. That’s about the same as Missouri overall (44.1%) and 2.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,172 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Perry County ranks #50 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
