Turnout in Koochiching County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,247 of Koochiching County’s 9,649 eligible citizens voted: 54.4% turnout. That’s 4.7 points lower than Minnesota overall (59.1%) and 8.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,402 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Koochiching County ranks #19 of 87 counties in Minnesota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
