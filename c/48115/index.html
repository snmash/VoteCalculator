Turnout in Dawson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,516 of Dawson County’s 8,557 eligible citizens voted: 29.4% turnout. That’s 11.4 points lower than Texas overall (40.8%) and 16.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,041 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 41.1%, so each ballot spoke for 2.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Dawson County ranks #15 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
