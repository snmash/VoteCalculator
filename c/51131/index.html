Turnout in Northampton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,434 of Northampton County’s 9,334 eligible citizens voted: 58.2% turnout. That’s 10.4 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 12.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,900 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Northampton County ranks #114 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
