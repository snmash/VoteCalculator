Turnout in Jackson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 685 of Jackson County’s 1,138 eligible citizens voted: 60.2% turnout. That’s 2.8 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 14.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 453 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jackson County ranks #26 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
