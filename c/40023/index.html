Turnout in Choctaw County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,050 of Choctaw County’s 10,623 eligible citizens voted: 38.1% turnout. That’s 1.3 points lower than Oklahoma overall (39.4%) and 7.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,573 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 53.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Choctaw County ranks #31 of 77 counties in Oklahoma for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
