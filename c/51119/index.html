Turnout in Middlesex County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,128 of Middlesex County’s 9,008 eligible citizens voted: 56.9% turnout. That’s 9.1 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 10.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,880 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Middlesex County ranks #112 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
