Nebraska voter turnout by county

Each vote in Nebraska counts for 2.1 people

47% of eligible Nebraska citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 668,836 of Nebraska’s 1,413,924 eligible citizens voted (47.3%), so each ballot spoke for 2.1 people. In 2024, turnout was 67.0% (1.5 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Dakota County (3.6 people per vote) and lowest in Loup County (1.4).
