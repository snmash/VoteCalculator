Turnout in Humboldt County

In the November 2022 midterm, 47,798 of Humboldt County’s 105,903 eligible citizens voted: 45.1% turnout. That’s 3.2 points higher than California overall (41.9%) and 0.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 58,105 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Humboldt County ranks #27 of 58 counties in California for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
