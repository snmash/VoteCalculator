Turnout in Custer County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,328 of Custer County’s 4,473 eligible citizens voted: 74.4% turnout. That’s 17.0 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 28.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,145 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 86.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Custer County ranks #60 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
