Turnout in Christian County

In the November 2022 midterm, 33,300 of Christian County’s 68,747 eligible citizens voted: 48.4% turnout. That’s 4.3 points higher than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 2.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 35,447 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 25.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Christian County ranks #87 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
