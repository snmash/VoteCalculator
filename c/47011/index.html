Turnout in Bradley County

In the November 2022 midterm, 24,669 of Bradley County’s 83,881 eligible citizens voted: 29.4% turnout. That’s 3.0 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 16.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 59,212 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 29.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bradley County ranks #28 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
