Turnout in Hot Springs County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,179 of Hot Springs County’s 3,559 eligible citizens voted: 61.2% turnout. That’s 17.0 points higher than Wyoming overall (44.2%) and 15.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,380 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hot Springs County ranks #23 of 23 counties in Wyoming for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
