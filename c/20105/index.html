Turnout in Lincoln County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,204 of Lincoln County’s 2,298 eligible citizens voted: 52.4% turnout. That’s 5.1 points higher than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 6.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,094 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lincoln County ranks #63 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
