Turnout in Lancaster County

In the November 2022 midterm, 114,001 of Lancaster County’s 241,442 eligible citizens voted: 47.2% turnout. That’s about the same as Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 1.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 127,441 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lancaster County ranks #22 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
