Turnout in Garden County

In the November 2022 midterm, 892 of Garden County’s 1,393 eligible citizens voted: 64.0% turnout. That’s 16.7 points higher than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 18.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 501 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 80.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Garden County ranks #85 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
