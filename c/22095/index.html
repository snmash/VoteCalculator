Turnout in St. John the Baptist Parish

In the November 2022 midterm, 11,643 of St. John the Baptist Parish’s 29,965 eligible citizens voted: 38.9% turnout. That’s 1.6 points lower than Louisiana overall (40.5%) and 7.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 18,322 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 62.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

St. John the Baptist Parish ranks #20 of 64 counties in Louisiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
