Turnout in Monroe County

In the November 2022 midterm, 58,827 of Monroe County’s 130,871 eligible citizens voted: 45.0% turnout. That’s 8.9 points lower than Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 1.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 72,044 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Monroe County ranks #3 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
