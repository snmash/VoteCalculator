Turnout in Hodgeman County

In the November 2022 midterm, 807 of Hodgeman County’s 1,217 eligible citizens voted: 66.3% turnout. That’s 19.0 points higher than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 20.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 410 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 79.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hodgeman County ranks #105 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
