Oregon voter turnout by county

Each vote in Oregon counts for 1.6 people

61% of eligible Oregon citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,952,842 of Oregon’s 3,211,678 eligible citizens voted (60.8%), so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. In 2024, turnout was 69.9% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Malheur County (2.4 people per vote) and lowest in Wallowa County (1.3).
