Turnout in Butler County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,066 of Butler County’s 10,901 eligible citizens voted: 55.6% turnout. That’s 4.3 points higher than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 9.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,835 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Butler County ranks #76 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
