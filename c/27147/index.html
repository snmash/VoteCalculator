Turnout in Steele County

In the November 2022 midterm, 16,539 of Steele County’s 27,567 eligible citizens voted: 60.0% turnout. That’s 0.9 points higher than Minnesota overall (59.1%) and 14.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 11,028 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Steele County ranks #59 of 87 counties in Minnesota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
