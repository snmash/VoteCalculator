Turnout in Campbell County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,471 of Campbell County’s 31,520 eligible citizens voted: 23.7% turnout. That’s 8.7 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 22.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 24,049 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 4.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 49.3%, so each ballot spoke for 2.0 people. Midterm turnout here ran 25.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Campbell County ranks #4 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
