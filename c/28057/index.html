Turnout in Itawamba County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,831 of Itawamba County’s 18,867 eligible citizens voted: 30.9% turnout. That’s 1.0 points lower than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 15.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 13,036 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 25.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Itawamba County ranks #20 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
