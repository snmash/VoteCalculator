California voter turnout by county

Each vote in California counts for 2.4 people

42% of eligible California citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 10,944,670 of California’s 26,132,814 eligible citizens voted (41.9%), so each ballot spoke for 2.4 people. In 2024, turnout was 60.7% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Kings County (3.5 people per vote) and lowest in Sierra County (1.5).
