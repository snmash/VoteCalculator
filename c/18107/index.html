Turnout in Montgomery County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,753 of Montgomery County’s 28,791 eligible citizens voted: 33.9% turnout. That’s 3.1 points lower than Indiana overall (37.0%) and 12.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 19,038 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Montgomery County ranks #16 of 92 counties in Indiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
