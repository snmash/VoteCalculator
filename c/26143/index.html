Turnout in Roscommon County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,957 of Roscommon County’s 20,205 eligible citizens voted: 64.1% turnout. That’s 6.0 points higher than Michigan overall (58.1%) and 18.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,248 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 79.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Roscommon County ranks #68 of 83 counties in Michigan for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
