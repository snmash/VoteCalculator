Turnout in Limestone County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,854 of Limestone County’s 15,832 eligible citizens voted: 43.3% turnout. That’s 2.5 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 2.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,978 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Limestone County ranks #119 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
