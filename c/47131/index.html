Turnout in Obion County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,695 of Obion County’s 23,242 eligible citizens voted: 33.1% turnout. That’s 0.7 points higher than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 12.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 15,547 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 55.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Obion County ranks #73 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
