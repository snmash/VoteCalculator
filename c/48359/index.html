Turnout in Oldham County

In the November 2022 midterm, 770 of Oldham County’s 1,298 eligible citizens voted: 59.3% turnout. That’s 18.5 points higher than Texas overall (40.8%) and 13.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 528 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Oldham County ranks #229 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
