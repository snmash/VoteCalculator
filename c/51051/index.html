Turnout in Dickenson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,897 of Dickenson County’s 11,106 eligible citizens voted: 35.1% turnout. That’s 12.7 points lower than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 10.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 7,209 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 28.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Dickenson County ranks #19 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
