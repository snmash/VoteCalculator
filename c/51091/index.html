Turnout in Highland County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,191 of Highland County’s 1,958 eligible citizens voted: 60.8% turnout. That’s 13.0 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 14.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 767 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 78.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Highland County ranks #128 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
