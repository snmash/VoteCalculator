Turnout in Putnam County

In the November 2022 midterm, 41,311 of Putnam County’s 74,322 eligible citizens voted: 55.6% turnout. That’s 13.7 points higher than New York overall (41.9%) and 9.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 33,011 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Putnam County ranks #60 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
