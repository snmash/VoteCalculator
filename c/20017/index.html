Turnout in Chase County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,185 of Chase County’s 1,982 eligible citizens voted: 59.8% turnout. That’s 12.5 points higher than Kansas overall (47.3%) and 13.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 797 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Chase County ranks #98 of 105 counties in Kansas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
