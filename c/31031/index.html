Turnout in Cherry County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,574 of Cherry County’s 4,093 eligible citizens voted: 62.9% turnout. That’s 15.6 points higher than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 16.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,519 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Cherry County ranks #84 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
