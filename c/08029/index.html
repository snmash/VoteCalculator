Turnout in Delta County

In the November 2022 midterm, 15,918 of Delta County’s 25,198 eligible citizens voted: 63.2% turnout. That’s 5.8 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 17.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,280 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Delta County ranks #35 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
