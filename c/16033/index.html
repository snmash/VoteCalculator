Turnout in Clark County

In the November 2022 midterm, 208 of Clark County’s 479 eligible citizens voted: 43.4% turnout. That’s 1.4 points higher than Idaho overall (42.0%) and 2.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 271 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clark County ranks #23 of 44 counties in Idaho for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
