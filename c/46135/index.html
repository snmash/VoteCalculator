Turnout in Yankton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 8,910 of Yankton County’s 17,986 eligible citizens voted: 49.5% turnout. That’s 2.8 points lower than South Dakota overall (52.3%) and 3.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,076 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Yankton County ranks #18 of 66 counties in South Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
