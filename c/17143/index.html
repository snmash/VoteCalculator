Turnout in Peoria County

In the November 2022 midterm, 59,034 of Peoria County’s 131,077 eligible citizens voted: 45.0% turnout. That’s about the same as Illinois overall (45.1%) and 1.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 72,043 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Peoria County ranks #18 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
