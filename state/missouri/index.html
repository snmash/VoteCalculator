Missouri voter turnout by county

Each vote in Missouri counts for 2.3 people

44% of eligible Missouri citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 2,068,355 of Missouri’s 4,685,995 eligible citizens voted (44.1%), so each ballot spoke for 2.3 people. In 2024, turnout was 63.9% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Pulaski County (4.4 people per vote) and lowest in Worth County (1.8).
