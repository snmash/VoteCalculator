Turnout in Winneshiek County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,677 of Winneshiek County’s 15,756 eligible citizens voted: 61.4% turnout. That’s 10.1 points higher than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 15.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,079 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Winneshiek County ranks #99 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
