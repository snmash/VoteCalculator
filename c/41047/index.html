Turnout in Marion County

In the November 2022 midterm, 134,850 of Marion County’s 239,085 eligible citizens voted: 56.4% turnout. That’s 4.4 points lower than Oregon overall (60.8%) and 10.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 104,235 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 9.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Marion County ranks #5 of 36 counties in Oregon for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
