Turnout in Bryan County

In the November 2022 midterm, 17,006 of Bryan County’s 33,578 eligible citizens voted: 50.6% turnout. That’s about the same as Georgia overall (50.5%) and 4.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 16,572 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 22.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bryan County ranks #102 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
