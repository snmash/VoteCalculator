Turnout in Chester County

In the November 2022 midterm, 258,267 of Chester County’s 403,082 eligible citizens voted: 64.1% turnout. That’s 10.2 points higher than Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 18.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 144,815 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 80.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Chester County ranks #67 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
