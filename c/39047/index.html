Turnout in Fayette County

In the November 2022 midterm, 8,483 of Fayette County’s 21,831 eligible citizens voted: 38.9% turnout. That’s 7.4 points lower than Ohio overall (46.3%) and 7.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 13,348 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Fayette County ranks #8 of 88 counties in Ohio for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
