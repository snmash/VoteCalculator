Turnout in Frontier County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,077 of Frontier County’s 2,006 eligible citizens voted: 53.7% turnout. That’s 6.4 points higher than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 7.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 929 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Frontier County ranks #50 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
