Turnout in Atascosa County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,706 of Atascosa County’s 34,999 eligible citizens voted: 36.3% turnout. That’s 4.5 points lower than Texas overall (40.8%) and 9.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 22,293 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 52.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Atascosa County ranks #54 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
