Turnout in Wise County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,315 of Wise County’s 28,414 eligible citizens voted: 32.8% turnout. That’s 15.0 points lower than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 13.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 19,099 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Wise County ranks #12 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
