Turnout in Hawaii County

In the November 2022 midterm, 64,022 of Hawaii County’s 152,477 eligible citizens voted: 42.0% turnout. That’s 2.8 points higher than Hawaii overall (39.2%) and 4.0 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 88,455 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 54.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hawaii County ranks #2 of 4 counties in Hawaii for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
