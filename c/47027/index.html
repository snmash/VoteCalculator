Turnout in Clay County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,729 of Clay County’s 6,209 eligible citizens voted: 27.8% turnout. That’s 4.6 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 18.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,480 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 32.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clay County ranks #19 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
