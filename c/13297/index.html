Turnout in Walton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 42,452 of Walton County’s 76,505 eligible citizens voted: 55.5% turnout. That’s 5.0 points higher than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 9.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 34,053 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Walton County ranks #128 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
