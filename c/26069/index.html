Turnout in Iosco County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,644 of Iosco County’s 20,911 eligible citizens voted: 60.5% turnout. That’s 2.4 points higher than Michigan overall (58.1%) and 14.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,267 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 75.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Iosco County ranks #54 of 83 counties in Michigan for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
