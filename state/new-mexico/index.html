New Mexico voter turnout by county

Each vote in New Mexico counts for 2.2 people

46% of eligible New Mexico citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 709,563 of New Mexico’s 1,553,240 eligible citizens voted (45.7%), so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. In 2024, turnout was 59.4% (1.7 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Curry County (3.1 people per vote) and lowest in Harding County (1.3).
