Turnout in Jefferson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 300,365 of Jefferson County’s 457,787 eligible citizens voted: 65.6% turnout. That’s 8.2 points higher than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 19.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 157,422 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 79.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jefferson County ranks #46 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
