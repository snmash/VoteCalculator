Turnout in Logan County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,148 of Logan County’s 24,887 eligible citizens voted: 24.7% turnout. That’s 8.8 points lower than West Virginia overall (33.5%) and 21.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 18,739 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 4.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 46.1%, so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Logan County ranks #2 of 55 counties in West Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
