Turnout in Hall County

In the November 2022 midterm, 15,167 of Hall County’s 39,300 eligible citizens voted: 38.6% turnout. That’s 8.7 points lower than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 7.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 24,133 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hall County ranks #5 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
