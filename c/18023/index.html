Turnout in Clinton County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,293 of Clinton County’s 21,800 eligible citizens voted: 33.5% turnout. That’s 3.5 points lower than Indiana overall (37.0%) and 12.5 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 14,507 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clinton County ranks #11 of 92 counties in Indiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
