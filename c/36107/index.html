Turnout in Tioga County

In the November 2022 midterm, 19,014 of Tioga County’s 37,793 eligible citizens voted: 50.3% turnout. That’s 8.4 points higher than New York overall (41.9%) and 4.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 18,779 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 65.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Tioga County ranks #46 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
