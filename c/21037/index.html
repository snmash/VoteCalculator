Turnout in Campbell County

In the November 2022 midterm, 35,664 of Campbell County’s 72,765 eligible citizens voted: 49.0% turnout. That’s 5.6 points higher than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 3.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 37,101 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 66.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Campbell County ranks #101 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
