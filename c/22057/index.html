Turnout in Lafourche Parish

In the November 2022 midterm, 30,177 of Lafourche Parish’s 72,966 eligible citizens voted: 41.4% turnout. That’s 0.9 points higher than Louisiana overall (40.5%) and 4.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 42,789 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 58.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lafourche Parish ranks #29 of 64 counties in Louisiana for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
