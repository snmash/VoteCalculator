Turnout in Calumet County

In the November 2022 midterm, 25,025 of Calumet County’s 40,322 eligible citizens voted: 62.1% turnout. That’s 3.1 points higher than Wisconsin overall (59.0%) and 16.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 15,297 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 81.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.2 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Calumet County ranks #61 of 72 counties in Wisconsin for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
