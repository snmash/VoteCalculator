Turnout in Lowndes County

In the November 2022 midterm, 34,760 of Lowndes County’s 88,242 eligible citizens voted: 39.4% turnout. That’s 11.1 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 6.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 53,482 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 54.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lowndes County ranks #16 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
