Turnout in Crowley County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,375 of Crowley County’s 4,848 eligible citizens voted: 28.4% turnout. That’s 29.0 points lower than Colorado overall (57.4%) and 17.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,473 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 35.2%, so each ballot spoke for 2.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 6.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Crowley County ranks #1 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
