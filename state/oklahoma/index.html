Oklahoma voter turnout by county

Each vote in Oklahoma counts for 2.5 people

39% of eligible Oklahoma citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,153,316 of Oklahoma’s 2,923,603 eligible citizens voted (39.4%), so each ballot spoke for 2.5 people. In 2024, turnout was 53.6% (1.9 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Comanche County (3.8 people per vote) and lowest in Dewey County (1.8).
