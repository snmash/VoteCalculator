Turnout in Guilford County

In the November 2022 midterm, 187,236 of Guilford County’s 398,605 eligible citizens voted: 47.0% turnout. That’s 0.8 points lower than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 1.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 211,369 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Guilford County ranks #45 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
