Turnout in Lewis County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,404 of Lewis County’s 13,090 eligible citizens voted: 33.6% turnout. That’s about the same as West Virginia overall (33.5%) and 12.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,686 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 53.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. Midterm turnout here ran 20.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lewis County ranks #27 of 55 counties in West Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
