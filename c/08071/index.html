Turnout in Las Animas County

In the November 2022 midterm, 6,673 of Las Animas County’s 11,520 eligible citizens voted: 57.9% turnout. That’s about the same as Colorado overall (57.4%) and 11.9 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,847 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 9.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Las Animas County ranks #21 of 64 counties in Colorado for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
