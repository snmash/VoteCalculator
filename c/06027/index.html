Turnout in Inyo County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,477 of Inyo County’s 14,190 eligible citizens voted: 52.7% turnout. That’s 10.8 points higher than California overall (41.9%) and 6.7 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,713 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 10.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Inyo County ranks #46 of 58 counties in California for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
