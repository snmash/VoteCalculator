Turnout in Pierce County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,851 of Pierce County’s 5,404 eligible citizens voted: 52.8% turnout. That’s 5.5 points higher than Nebraska overall (47.3%) and 6.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,553 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 19.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pierce County ranks #46 of 93 counties in Nebraska for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
