Turnout in Lyon County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,800 of Lyon County’s 18,101 eligible citizens voted: 54.1% turnout. That’s 5.0 points lower than Minnesota overall (59.1%) and 8.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 8,301 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 71.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lyon County ranks #17 of 87 counties in Minnesota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
