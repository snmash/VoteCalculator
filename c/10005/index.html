Turnout in Sussex County

In the November 2022 midterm, 98,030 of Sussex County’s 199,003 eligible citizens voted: 49.3% turnout. That’s 7.2 points higher than Delaware overall (42.1%) and 3.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 100,973 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sussex County ranks #3 of 3 counties in Delaware for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
