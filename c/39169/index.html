Turnout in Wayne County

In the November 2022 midterm, 38,607 of Wayne County’s 87,142 eligible citizens voted: 44.3% turnout. That’s 2.0 points lower than Ohio overall (46.3%) and 1.7 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 48,535 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 61.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Wayne County ranks #36 of 88 counties in Ohio for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
