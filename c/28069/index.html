Turnout in Kemper County

In the November 2022 midterm, 2,387 of Kemper County’s 7,111 eligible citizens voted: 33.6% turnout. That’s 1.7 points higher than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 12.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,724 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 23.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Kemper County ranks #39 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
