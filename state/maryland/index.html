Maryland voter turnout by county

Each vote in Maryland counts for 2.2 people

45% of eligible Maryland citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 2,007,015 of Maryland’s 4,418,316 eligible citizens voted (45.4%), so each ballot spoke for 2.2 people. In 2024, turnout was 68.8% (1.5 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Baltimore city (3.0 people per vote) and lowest in Talbot County (1.7).
