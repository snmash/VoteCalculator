Turnout in Jackson County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,061 of Jackson County’s 9,804 eligible citizens voted: 31.2% turnout. That’s 1.2 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 14.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 6,743 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Jackson County ranks #47 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
