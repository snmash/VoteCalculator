Turnout in Bedford County

In the November 2022 midterm, 33,688 of Bedford County’s 64,205 eligible citizens voted: 52.5% turnout. That’s 4.7 points higher than Virginia overall (47.8%) and 6.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 30,517 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 79.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Bedford County ranks #98 of 133 counties in Virginia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
