Turnout in Coryell County

In the November 2022 midterm, 16,379 of Coryell County’s 63,227 eligible citizens voted: 25.9% turnout. That’s 14.9 points lower than Texas overall (40.8%) and 20.1 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 46,848 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 37.8%, so each ballot spoke for 2.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Coryell County ranks #3 of 254 counties in Texas for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
