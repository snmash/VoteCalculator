Turnout in Baraga County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,444 of Baraga County’s 6,781 eligible citizens voted: 50.8% turnout. That’s 7.3 points lower than Michigan overall (58.1%) and 4.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,337 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.9%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 13.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Baraga County ranks #11 of 83 counties in Michigan for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
