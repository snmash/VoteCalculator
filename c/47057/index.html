Turnout in Grainger County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,381 of Grainger County’s 19,486 eligible citizens voted: 27.6% turnout. That’s 4.8 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 18.4 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 14,105 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.6 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 29.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Grainger County ranks #18 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
