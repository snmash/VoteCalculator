Turnout in Christian County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,430 of Christian County’s 26,891 eligible citizens voted: 46.2% turnout. That’s 1.1 points higher than Illinois overall (45.1%) and about the same as the national rate (46.0%). With 14,461 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.6 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Christian County ranks #27 of 102 counties in Illinois for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
