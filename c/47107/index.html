Turnout in McMinn County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,803 of McMinn County’s 42,601 eligible citizens voted: 30.1% turnout. That’s 2.3 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 15.9 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 29,798 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 26.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

McMinn County ranks #36 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
