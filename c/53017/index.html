Turnout in Douglas County

In the November 2022 midterm, 16,117 of Douglas County’s 28,541 eligible citizens voted: 56.5% turnout. That’s 1.9 points higher than Washington overall (54.6%) and 10.5 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 12,424 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Douglas County ranks #20 of 39 counties in Washington for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
