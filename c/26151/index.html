Turnout in Sanilac County

In the November 2022 midterm, 18,813 of Sanilac County’s 31,792 eligible citizens voted: 59.2% turnout. That’s 1.1 points higher than Michigan overall (58.1%) and 13.2 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 12,979 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 73.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sanilac County ranks #46 of 83 counties in Michigan for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
