Turnout in Story County

In the November 2022 midterm, 36,972 of Story County’s 79,012 eligible citizens voted: 46.8% turnout. That’s 4.5 points lower than Iowa overall (51.3%) and 0.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 42,040 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Story County ranks #16 of 99 counties in Iowa for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
