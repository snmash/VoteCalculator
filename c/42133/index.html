Turnout in York County

In the November 2022 midterm, 186,551 of York County’s 354,597 eligible citizens voted: 52.6% turnout. That’s 1.3 points lower than Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 6.6 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 168,046 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

York County ranks #36 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
