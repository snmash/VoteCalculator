Turnout in Noxubee County

In the November 2022 midterm, 3,045 of Noxubee County’s 7,461 eligible citizens voted: 40.8% turnout. That’s 8.9 points higher than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 5.2 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 4,416 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 59.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Noxubee County ranks #72 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
