Tennessee voter turnout by county

Each vote in Tennessee counts for 3.1 people

32% of eligible Tennessee citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,710,698 of Tennessee’s 5,275,356 eligible citizens voted (32.4%), so each ballot spoke for 3.1 people. In 2024, turnout was 58.1% (1.7 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Lake County (5.0 people per vote) and lowest in Williamson County (2.2).
