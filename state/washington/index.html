Washington voter turnout by county

Each vote in Washington counts for 1.8 people

55% of eligible Washington citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 3,046,644 of Washington’s 5,583,131 eligible citizens voted (54.6%), so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. In 2024, turnout was 70.3% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Franklin County (2.5 people per vote) and lowest in San Juan County (1.4).
