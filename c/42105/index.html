Turnout in Potter County

In the November 2022 midterm, 7,108 of Potter County’s 12,864 eligible citizens voted: 55.3% turnout. That’s 1.4 points higher than Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 9.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 5,756 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 70.6%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Potter County ranks #50 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
