Rhode Island voter turnout by county

Each vote in Rhode Island counts for 2.3 people

43% of eligible Rhode Island citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 357,968 of Rhode Island’s 826,626 eligible citizens voted (43.3%), so each ballot spoke for 2.3 people. In 2024, turnout was 61.9% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Providence County (2.7 people per vote) and lowest in Washington County (1.8).
