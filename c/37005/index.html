Turnout in Alleghany County

In the November 2022 midterm, 5,031 of Alleghany County’s 9,012 eligible citizens voted: 55.8% turnout. That’s 8.0 points higher than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 9.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 3,981 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 72.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Alleghany County ranks #88 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
