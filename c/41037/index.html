Turnout in Lake County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,008 of Lake County’s 6,649 eligible citizens voted: 60.3% turnout. That’s 0.5 points lower than Oregon overall (60.8%) and 14.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 2,641 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 3.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lake County ranks #12 of 36 counties in Oregon for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
