Turnout in Clay County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,925 of Clay County’s 15,679 eligible citizens voted: 31.4% turnout. That’s 12.0 points lower than Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 14.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 10,754 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.2 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 47.9%, so each ballot spoke for 2.1 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Clay County ranks #4 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
