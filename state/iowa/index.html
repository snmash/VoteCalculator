Iowa voter turnout by county

Each vote in Iowa counts for 1.9 people

51% of eligible Iowa citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,222,460 of Iowa’s 2,381,736 eligible citizens voted (51.3%), so each ballot spoke for 1.9 people. In 2024, turnout was 70.3% (1.4 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Woodbury County (2.5 people per vote) and lowest in Winneshiek County (1.6).
