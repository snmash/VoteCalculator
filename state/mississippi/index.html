Mississippi voter turnout by county

Each vote in Mississippi counts for 3.1 people

32% of eligible Mississippi citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 709,241 of Mississippi’s 2,221,778 eligible citizens voted (31.9%), so each ballot spoke for 3.1 people. In 2024, turnout was 55.3% (1.8 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Tunica County (4.9 people per vote) and lowest in Issaquena County (1.5).
