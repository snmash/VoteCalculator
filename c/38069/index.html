Turnout in Pierce County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,495 of Pierce County’s 3,107 eligible citizens voted: 48.1% turnout. That’s 6.8 points higher than North Dakota overall (41.3%) and 2.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 1,612 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pierce County ranks #21 of 53 counties in North Dakota for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
