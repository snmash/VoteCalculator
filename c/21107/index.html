Turnout in Hopkins County

In the November 2022 midterm, 14,797 of Hopkins County’s 34,127 eligible citizens voted: 43.4% turnout. That’s about the same as Kentucky overall (43.4%) and 2.6 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 19,330 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 60.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.8 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hopkins County ranks #64 of 120 counties in Kentucky for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
