Turnout in Ulster County

In the November 2022 midterm, 78,069 of Ulster County’s 146,155 eligible citizens voted: 53.4% turnout. That’s 11.5 points higher than New York overall (41.9%) and 7.4 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 68,086 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 67.4%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Ulster County ranks #57 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
