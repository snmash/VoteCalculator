Kansas voter turnout by county

Each vote in Kansas counts for 2.1 people

47% of eligible Kansas citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 1,009,241 of Kansas’s 2,134,496 eligible citizens voted (47.3%), so each ballot spoke for 2.1 people. In 2024, turnout was 62.2% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Seward County (3.9 people per vote) and lowest in Hodgeman County (1.5).
