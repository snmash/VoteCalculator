Turnout in Wake County

In the November 2022 midterm, 453,170 of Wake County’s 824,675 eligible citizens voted: 55.0% turnout. That’s 7.2 points higher than North Carolina overall (47.8%) and 9.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 371,505 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 79.3%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Wake County ranks #86 of 100 counties in North Carolina for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
