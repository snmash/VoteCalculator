Turnout in Creek County

In the November 2022 midterm, 22,350 of Creek County’s 55,186 eligible citizens voted: 40.5% turnout. That’s 1.1 points higher than Oklahoma overall (39.4%) and 5.5 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 32,836 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 56.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 16.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Creek County ranks #44 of 77 counties in Oklahoma for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
