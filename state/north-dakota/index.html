North Dakota voter turnout by county

Each vote in North Dakota counts for 2.4 people

41% of eligible North Dakota citizens voted in the 2022 midterm.

In the 2022 midterm, 240,143 of North Dakota’s 581,723 eligible citizens voted (41.3%), so each ballot spoke for 2.4 people. In 2024, turnout was 63.3% (1.6 people per vote). Nationally, 2022 turnout was 46.0%. Vote power is highest in Sioux County (4.2 people per vote) and lowest in Nelson County (1.5).
