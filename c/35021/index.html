Turnout in Harding County

In the November 2022 midterm, 413 of Harding County’s 551 eligible citizens voted: 75.0% turnout. That’s 29.3 points higher than New Mexico overall (45.7%) and 29.0 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 138 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.3 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 2.1 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Harding County ranks #33 of 33 counties in New Mexico for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
