Turnout in Hancock County

In the November 2022 midterm, 12,223 of Hancock County’s 36,810 eligible citizens voted: 33.2% turnout. That’s 1.3 points higher than Mississippi overall (31.9%) and 12.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 24,587 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 57.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.7 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.3 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Hancock County ranks #37 of 82 counties in Mississippi for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
