Turnout in Polk County

In the November 2022 midterm, 20,454 of Polk County’s 36,299 eligible citizens voted: 56.3% turnout. That’s 2.7 points lower than Wisconsin overall (59.0%) and 10.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 15,845 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 21.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Polk County ranks #27 of 72 counties in Wisconsin for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
