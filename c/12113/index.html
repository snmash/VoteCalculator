Turnout in Santa Rosa County

In the November 2022 midterm, 75,698 of Santa Rosa County’s 151,995 eligible citizens voted: 49.8% turnout. That’s 1.8 points higher than Florida overall (48.0%) and 3.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 76,297 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.0 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 74.0%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 24.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Santa Rosa County ranks #37 of 67 counties in Florida for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
