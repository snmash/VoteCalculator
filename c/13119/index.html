Turnout in Franklin County

In the November 2022 midterm, 8,739 of Franklin County’s 18,183 eligible citizens voted: 48.1% turnout. That’s 2.4 points lower than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 2.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,444 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.1 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 66.8%, so each ballot spoke for 1.5 people. Midterm turnout here ran 18.7 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Franklin County ranks #80 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
