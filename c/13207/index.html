Turnout in Monroe County

In the November 2022 midterm, 13,718 of Monroe County’s 23,147 eligible citizens voted: 59.3% turnout. That’s 8.8 points higher than Georgia overall (50.5%) and 13.3 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,429 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 76.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 17.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Monroe County ranks #143 of 159 counties in Georgia for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
