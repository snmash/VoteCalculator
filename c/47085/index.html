Turnout in Humphreys County

In the November 2022 midterm, 4,451 of Humphreys County’s 14,965 eligible citizens voted: 29.7% turnout. That’s 2.7 points lower than Tennessee overall (32.4%) and 16.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 10,514 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 3.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 55.2%, so each ballot spoke for 1.8 people. Midterm turnout here ran 25.5 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Humphreys County ranks #33 of 95 counties in Tennessee for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
