Turnout in Garfield County

In the November 2022 midterm, 16,214 of Garfield County’s 43,578 eligible citizens voted: 37.2% turnout. That’s 2.2 points lower than Oklahoma overall (39.4%) and 8.8 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 27,364 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 2.7 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 50.1%, so each ballot spoke for 2.0 people. Midterm turnout here ran 12.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Garfield County ranks #23 of 77 counties in Oklahoma for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
