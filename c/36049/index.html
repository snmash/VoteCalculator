Turnout in Lewis County

In the November 2022 midterm, 10,655 of Lewis County’s 20,434 eligible citizens voted: 52.1% turnout. That’s 10.2 points higher than New York overall (41.9%) and 6.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 9,779 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.9 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 63.5%, so each ballot spoke for 1.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 11.4 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Lewis County ranks #54 of 62 counties in New York for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
