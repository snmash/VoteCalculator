Turnout in Pulaski County

In the November 2022 midterm, 9,320 of Pulaski County’s 40,999 eligible citizens voted: 22.7% turnout. That’s 21.4 points lower than Missouri overall (44.1%) and 23.3 points lower than the national rate (46.0%). With 31,679 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 4.4 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 37.9%, so each ballot spoke for 2.6 people. Midterm turnout here ran 15.2 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Pulaski County ranks #1 of 115 counties in Missouri for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
