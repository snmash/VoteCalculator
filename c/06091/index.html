Turnout in Sierra County

In the November 2022 midterm, 1,553 of Sierra County’s 2,256 eligible citizens voted: 68.8% turnout. That’s 26.9 points higher than California overall (41.9%) and 22.8 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 703 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.5 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.7%, so each ballot spoke for 1.3 people. Midterm turnout here ran 8.9 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Sierra County ranks #58 of 58 counties in California for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
