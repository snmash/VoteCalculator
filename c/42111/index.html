Turnout in Somerset County

In the November 2022 midterm, 32,576 of Somerset County’s 59,155 eligible citizens voted: 55.1% turnout. That’s 1.2 points higher than Pennsylvania overall (53.9%) and 9.1 points higher than the national rate (46.0%). With 26,579 eligible citizens sitting it out, each ballot cast spoke for 1.8 people.

In the 2024 presidential election, turnout was 69.1%, so each ballot spoke for 1.4 people. Midterm turnout here ran 14.0 points below the presidential year, which is why every vote in a midterm like 2026 carries extra weight.

Somerset County ranks #48 of 67 counties in Pennsylvania for vote power (#1 has the lowest turnout, so each vote there counts for the most people).
